# Public social/community research

Social/community sources can provide buyer language, complaints, workflows and trend signals, but are noisy evidence. Use public/authorized read-only access only.

Default UberBond rule:
- no account login automation;
- no browser-cookie/private-session harvesting;
- no posting/commenting/liking/messaging;
- no CAPTCHA/access-control bypass;
- no inference of private contact information.

If a platform requires authentication to read the requested material, stop at `REVIEW` unless a separate owner-authorized mission and current policy review permit that exact access. Community claims remain anecdotes until corroborated.
