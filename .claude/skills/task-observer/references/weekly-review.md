# Weekly observation review

Adapted from Eoghan Henn / rebelytics.com, CC BY 4.0, pinned source `510caad26c907793e48306262af216ff9f71c9f7`.

Review only real observations. Do not manufacture a backlog to justify skill work.

1. Scan open observation frontmatter, then read full bodies for candidates being acted on.
2. Group by existing skill, proposed skill and cross-cutting principle.
3. Check duplicates, contradictions, sibling skills and whether current repo state has already solved the issue.
4. Rank by recurrence, severity, founder-minute reduction, economic leverage, safety and evidence quality.
5. For each proposed change classify: additive fix, structural skill revision, new skill, cross-cutting principle, decline, supersede, or park.
6. Before editing/creating skills, load `skill-authoring.md`.
7. Stage substantial changes for review/testing. Do not silently mutate canonical policy or company truth.
8. Verify the changed skill against representative tasks and counterexamples.
9. Update observation statuses and resolutions in the same review.
10. Update `last-review-date.txt` only after the review actually completes.

UberBond rule: a skill-improvement review is subordinate to current commercial priorities. Do not spend a whole autonomous shift polishing prompts while a higher-value dependency-satisfied payment, distribution, delivery or liveness mission is available.
