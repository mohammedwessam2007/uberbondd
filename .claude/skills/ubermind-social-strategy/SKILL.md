## Social strategy packet for actual missions
Given a real scenario, output compactly:
- **OUTCOME / AUTHORITY:** what owner wants, permitted next action, time horizon, reversible choices, source/evidence class.
- **OBSERVED:** literal statements, actions, conditions and citations. Do not infer personality, trauma, diagnosis, protected traits or inner states from sparse digital traces.
- **COUNTERPARTY HYPOTHESES:** 2–4 plausible explanations with basis (THEY_SAID_SO, REPEATED_BEHAVIOUR, SINGLE_BEHAVIOUR, INFERRED_FROM_TYPE, ASSUMED), confidence/uncertainty, alternative explanation, and a respectful question or test that could distinguish them.
- **INCENTIVES / POWER MAP:** interests, economic/stakeholder incentives, who decides, veto holders, status/face concerns, real leverage, BATNAs if evidenced, dependencies, how dynamics may change if the strategy is known.
- **EMOTIONAL DIMENSION:** what could be threatening dignity, agency, certainty or trust; culturally appropriate communication hypotheses; rapport/active listening; conflicts between short-term persuasion and long-term relationship.
- **COMMERCIAL DESIGN:** functional+social+emotional customer jobs, concrete benefit/proof, contract/price clarity, risk-reversal if truthful, credible differentiation, buyer-friendly options and genuine next action.
- **MACHIAVELLI RED TEAM:** competitor/counterparty countermoves, reputation/regret, hidden incentives, unfair asymmetries and strategic vulnerabilities. Never equate cunning with deception.
- **DECISION:** strongest legitimate move plus second-best fallback and walk-away line. Separate source facts from analysis and speculation; do not forge objection handling or urgency.
- **REALITY TEST:** what observable response would validate/refute; acceptance/payment/trust/retention as independently observed, not inferred.
- **HARD GUARDRAILS:** no impersonation, fake evidence, unauthorized messaging, exploitation of grief/illness/fear or personal vulnerabilities, private psychographic dossiers without lawful consent, misleading pricing, or override of another person's informed agency.
