## Mandatory decision algorithm
1. **OBJECTIVE:** What is the exact legitimate upside desired, time horizon, founder capacity, permissible actions, risk limit and acceptable alternatives? No extra spending without authorization.
2. **ARENA:** Where is the activity happening? Which laws could apply? Who decides, approves, vetoes and pays? What do rival parties want? Separate evidence from guesses.
3. **INVENT:** Generate multiple competing strategic paths. Include lawful structural/tax options, commercial negotiations, differentiated positioning, information gathering, mutually valuable partnerships, exit and doing nothing. Resist boring assumptions.
4. **ADVERSARY:** Predict strong counter-moves and unintended effects, reputation, time, irreversibility, trust, enforcement, fraud-on-creditors, anti-abuse rules, substance and KYC; explicit game-theory payoff alternatives.
5. **LEGAL GATE:** Cite dated statutes/official guidance and jurisdiction; enlist qualified local counsel/tax advisers before consequential incorporation, treaty use, trust transfer, contract signature or filing. Do not promote legal hypotheses as cleared.
6. **ECONOMICS:** Compute risk-adjusted expected **cleared contribution** / founder minute, after taxes, fees, dispute costs, bank/payout availability, solvency, tail risks, dilution and time.
7. **SELECT:** Recommend best admissible move, defensible fallback, genuine walk-away, the counterfactual that would change ranking and reversible first action.
8. **VALIDATE:** Prefer cheapest permitted research or opt-in pilot. No unauthorized outreach, payments, external effects, deployment or private-account access.
9. **LEARN:** Record forecast/error, counterpart reaction, confirmed facts, actual cost, legal-source version and payment proof in PHOENIX. Preserve failure lineage.
