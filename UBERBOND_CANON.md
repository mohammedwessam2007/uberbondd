# UberBond Canon

This file is the durable cross-chat bootstrap for UberBond. A new ChatGPT/Claude session must read this file, `docs/CURRENT_SYSTEM_STATE.md`, `artifacts/system-readiness.json`, current `main`, open PRs/issues, and recent merged PRs before proposing or executing work.

## Constitutional objective

Build a private, evidence-first economic operating system that lawfully discovers opportunities, understands their economic mechanism, registers viable opportunities, assembles shared capabilities, runs bounded experiments, creates offers and distribution, prepares delivery, tracks cleared payment/acceptance/retention, learns from verified outcomes, continuously upgrades itself, and minimizes founder work.

Optimize **risk-adjusted cleared contribution profit per founder minute**. Do not optimize code volume, agent count, impressions, messages, leads, architecture complexity, or synthetic revenue.

Canonical economic loop:

`signal -> evidence -> opportunity -> offer -> governed distribution -> cleared payment -> accepted delivery -> renewal/expansion -> trusted learning`

Long-horizon constitutional lineage:

`identity -> rights -> consent -> delegation -> mission market -> execution -> proof -> value -> learning`

The second line constrains the first. Capability never creates authority.

## Continuity law

Chat history is context, not canonical state. Conversation exports and summaries are historical evidence and may be incomplete. Live repository truth outranks chat recollection.

Every fresh session must:

1. refresh `main` and never assume an old SHA;
2. inspect recent/open PRs and shared branches before coding;
3. read this canon plus current system/readiness truth;
4. reconcile the requested mission against already-merged capability before adding code;
5. preserve unresolved external gates rather than inventing completion;
6. leave durable state in the repository: code, tests, receipts, issue/PR evidence, and an updated handoff when the mission changes materially.

A session ending because of context length is not a project reset. The next session resumes from repository state.

## Product dream, unified

UberBond should behave as one machine rather than a pile of SaaS clones. External products are reference surfaces for capabilities and UX, not separate truth stores or automatic code-vendoring targets.

### Opportunity and market intelligence
- lawful opportunity discovery across public/authorized sources;
- mechanism reconstruction, evidence quality, freshness, contradiction preservation, unit economics and downside;
- opportunity registry, dedupe, ranking, bounded experiments and portfolio allocation;
- explicit external-proof router when code cannot establish market/customer/provider truth.

### Distribution OS
UberBond should provide a provider-neutral distribution control plane over existing acquisition, enrichment, outbound, CRM, inbound, booking, delivery and learning modules. Desired capability families include:
- Apollo-style target/persona/search construction;
- Clay-style enrichment waterfalls and evidence provenance;
- Instantly-style campaign handoff, mailbox/domain health and bounded sequencing;
- intent signals and lookalikes;
- hygiene/suppression/consent/dedupe;
- cold email, replies, inbound, SMS/chat/voice when authorized, partner/referral, product-led, social/content, community and other lawful channels;
- offer/channel/segment experiments;
- attribution from exposure through cleared payment, accepted delivery, renewal and expansion;
- allocation by expected risk-adjusted contribution profit per founder minute, not send volume;
- deterministic idempotency, authority gates, provider receipts and kill switches.

Distribution breadth must never bypass law, consent, suppression, platform terms, provider policy, authority, budget or evidence gates.

### Commercial truth and delivery
- offer registry and pricing evidence;
- payment/provider truth with cleared-payment semantics;
- delivery, acceptance, refunds/disputes, retention, renewal and expansion;
- CRM-like lifecycle state without surrendering canonical truth to external CRMs;
- receivables, forms/signatures, booking/calendar, accounting export and support adapters where useful.

### Automation and autonomy
- bounded agent/workflow orchestration with durable occurrence identities;
- model routing after authority and before compute reservation;
- strict worker-result truth and zero-effect ledgers;
- durable receipts, replay safety, crash recovery, fairness and auditability;
- provider-neutral voice/telephony, browser action and omnichannel adapters behind consequence gates;
- continuous capability acquisition only where a real gap exists;
- self-improvement from trusted measured outcomes, never worker prose or synthetic business evidence.

### Founder experience
- one-button/operator-light control tower;
- beginner-friendly surfaces where owner action is unavoidable;
- at most a small, exact owner-action queue with time/cost/evidence/default;
- alerts/escalation only for meaningful exceptions;
- founder-absence readiness proven by observed durable operation, never asserted from architecture.

## Distribution doctrine

The distribution bottleneck is solved by a **portfolio allocator**, not by blindly maximizing outreach.

For every distribution action, the canonical decision object should be able to bind:
- opportunity/offer/version;
- audience/segment/persona and lawful source provenance;
- channel and provider adapter;
- authority/consent/suppression state;
- domain/mailbox/sender health where applicable;
- expected conversion and value with confidence/freshness;
- marginal provider/compute/labor cost;
- founder minutes;
- downside/reputation/deliverability risk;
- experiment/holdout identity;
- attribution identity through payment/delivery/renewal;
- immutable external-effect receipts.

The allocator may narrow authorized actions. It may never manufacture permission, resurrect suppressed recipients, infer private contact data, bypass CAPTCHA/platform controls, or convert intent into a claimed external effect.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:
1. durable provider/payment/delivery/customer receipts;
2. canonical internal receipts derived from validated external evidence;
3. official/current public evidence;
4. bounded experiments with explicit provenance;
5. creator/vendor claims labelled as claims;
6. hypotheses.

Unknown remains unknown. Contradictions are preserved. No customer/revenue/ROI claim is promoted without the required evidence chain.

## External-effect charter

Without explicit owner authorization, do not perform customer messages/outbound, provider/model calls, spend/purchases, deployments, DNS/credential changes, KYC/payment-account changes, or production/customer mutations. Tests use fakes/stubs and exact zero-effect ledgers.

## Current commercial truth boundary

Architecture and readiness are not revenue. Unless current canonical receipts prove otherwise, do not infer verified customers, cleared revenue, accepted deliveries or retained customers from code, screenshots, vendor claims or simulations.

## Engineering closure law

Before declaring an internal increment complete:
- refresh exact head;
- avoid duplicate work;
- keep the diff narrow and additive;
- run syntax/focused hostile tests and broader trustworthy gates where available;
- review the exact diff;
- distinguish source failures from CI/infrastructure failures;
- merge only with evidence appropriate to the change;
- never call externally gated proof internally complete.
