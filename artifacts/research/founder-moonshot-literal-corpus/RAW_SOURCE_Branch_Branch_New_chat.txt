The IQ numbers are fictional power levels, not psychometrics. The rule here is stricter than before: **if an idea could merely become a big company, it does not qualify. It should be capable, in principle, of creating whole descendants of science, technology, or civilization.**

1. **THE CAUSAL COMPILER**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁴**  
   Instead of programming machines, program **cause-and-effect chains**. Give it an end state and it discovers the minimum chain of interventions that would make that state emerge across physical, biological, economic, and computational systems. Programming graduates from `instructions → output` to **`desired reality → causal architecture → reality`**.

2. **THE NEW CONSTANT HUNTER**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁴**  
   Search huge bodies of observation for quantities that appear invariant but have never been recognized as fundamental. Discovering a genuinely new conservation law, scaling relation, or invariant could open an entire science the way conservation of energy did.

3. **THE MECHANISM TELESCOPE**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁵**  
   Current science sees phenomena and tries to infer mechanisms. This system decomposes every observed process into universal mechanism fragments and searches for the hidden machinery connecting them. Eventually humanity might possess a **periodic table of mechanisms**.

4. **THE COUNTERFACTUAL MICROSCOPE**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁵**  
   A microscope reveals what exists at smaller scales. This reveals what **would have existed under minimally different histories**. For every event it maps the nearby alternate causal universes, allowing science to investigate causation almost as concretely as structure.

5. **THE THEORY BREEDER**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁶**  
   Scientific theories become evolving organisms. They reproduce, mutate assumptions, exchange mathematical structures, face adversarial experiments, and die when predictions fail. Science becomes an **evolutionary ecosystem of explanations** rather than a sequence of papers.

6. **THE AXIOM MUTATOR**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁶**  
   Instead of finding theorems from fixed axioms, mutate the axioms themselves. Search mathematical universes for systems whose structure unexpectedly maps onto reality. This could discover mathematics that no human would have considered worth inventing.

7. **THE MATHEMATICAL PARTICLE ACCELERATOR**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁷**  
   Smash distant mathematical structures together and examine what survives the collision. Category theory × topology × probability × dynamical systems × unknown generated formal systems. Most combinations die. A few might produce entirely new mathematical matter.

8. **THE REPRESENTATION SINGULARITY**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁷**  
   Many hard problems are hard because we encode them badly. This system searches representations themselves until impossible-looking problems become trivial. Instead of faster algorithms, you get **new ways reality can be represented**.

9. **THE DIMENSION INVENTOR**  
   **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁸**  
   Search for useful abstract dimensions that humans have not conceived. Not spatial dimensions necessarily, but measurable axes along which systems become simple. A hidden dimension might unify diseases, markets, minds, or materials that currently look unrelated.

10. **THE QUESTION GENERATOR**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁸**  
    Civilization has enormous machinery for answering questions but almost none for systematically discovering **the questions whose answers would reorganize civilization**. This engine optimizes question selection itself. One extraordinary question could be worth more than a million competent answers.

11. **THE EXPERIMENT GENERATOR**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10³⁹**  
    Instead of humans designing experiments, continuously generate the experiment that maximally separates competing models of reality. Science becomes an automated dialogue where every experiment is selected for maximum epistemic destruction.

12. **THE INSTRUMENT INVENTOR**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10³⁹**  
    Entire sciences often appear after a new instrument: telescope, microscope, particle accelerator, sequencer. This engine asks what phenomena humanity cannot discover because **the necessary instrument does not yet exist**, then invents that instrument first.

13. **THE OBSERVABILITY ENGINE**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10⁴⁰**  
    Some phenomena may remain mysterious simply because the variables that matter cannot be observed. This system searches for ways to convert hidden state into measurable state. Its product is not knowledge, but **making previously unknowable things knowable**.

14. **THE MEASUREMENT COMPILER**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10⁴⁰**  
    Give it a concept such as consciousness, organizational resilience, cellular aging, or scientific creativity. It attempts to construct valid operational measurements from first principles rather than relying on crude proxies. Whole domains could become scientific once their central quantities become measurable.

15. **THE UNCERTAINTY ENGINE**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10⁴¹**  
    Instead of merely estimating uncertainty, treat uncertainty itself as a resource map. It finds where one bit of new information would unlock the largest cascade of decisions. Civilization starts spending experiments where ignorance is most expensive.

16. **THE ANOMALY CIVILIZATION**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10⁴¹**  
    Build an entire scientific institution around anomalies rather than established disciplines. Every unexplained residual, failed replication, strange outlier, contradictory experiment, and “impossible” measurement becomes a first-class research target. Revolutions often begin in the leftovers.

17. **THE FAILURE MINE**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10⁴²**  
    Humanity throws away astonishing quantities of failed research. This system treats failure as structured information and searches billions of unsuccessful attempts for hidden regularities. The graveyard of ideas becomes a new scientific dataset.

18. **THE NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE GRAPH**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10⁴²**  
    Record not merely what works, but precisely **what cannot work under which assumptions and why**. Over centuries this could stop civilization from repeatedly rediscovering the same dead ends and reveal the boundaries surrounding unexplored territory.

19. **THE IMPOSSIBILITY CLASSIFIER**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10⁴³**  
    Every “impossible” goal is decomposed into: forbidden by physics, forbidden by mathematics, limited by engineering, limited by cost, limited by knowledge, limited by coordination, or merely never tried. Humanity gains a map separating **true impossibility from temporary ignorance**.

20. **THE BARRIER DESTROYER**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 3 × 10⁴³**  
    Instead of attacking goals directly, identify the smallest bottleneck whose removal unlocks thousands of downstream technologies. The steam engine attacked mechanical power. Transistors attacked switching. This engine hunts the next civilization-scale bottleneck.

21. **THE ADJACENT-POSSIBLE RADAR**  
    **Hypothetical IQ: 10⁴⁴**  
    Continuously map everything that has become newly feasible because of the latest discoveries. A paper published this morning might make an invention possible that was impossible yesterday. UberBond notices immediately.

22. **THE POSSIBILITY SUPPLY CHAIN**

23. **THE DISCOVERY ROUTER**

38. **THE CIVILIZATION RED TEAM**
