# UberBond Agent-Native Revenue Architecture

Date: 2026-09-12

## Purpose

Fuse two external mechanisms into UberBond without importing their claims as truth:

1. Multi-stage candidate generation, rejection, scoring, limit-setting, memory, and independent veto.
2. Agent-native product access where software can be discovered and operated through machine-readable commands rather than requiring a human UI.

## Revenue Decision Desk

The desk is a bounded tournament, not an autonomous permission grant.

PROBE generates candidates.
PRISM rejects weak evidence.
SCALE ranks by risk-adjusted cleared contribution profit per founder minute.
LIMIT requires stop conditions and bounds before execution.
ARCHIVE prevents repeated bad ideas from silently re-entering without new evidence.
EINSTEIN is an independent veto that can red-light a candidate even when projected upside is large.

The do-nothing baseline always remains available.

## Agent-Native Commerce Surface

UberBond products should be usable by both humans and authorized agents.

The surface provides:

- machine-readable product and command discovery;
- stable revision hashes so agents can detect interface drift;
- structured JSON outputs and meaningful exit codes;
- delegated identity tied to a human or organization principal;
- attenuation-only child delegation;
- explicit scopes;
- bounded spend metadata where applicable;
- expiry;
- dry-run-first policy for destructive actions;
- receipts that bind command, principal, agent, input hash, mode, and interface revision.

## Commercial consequence

The acquisition funnel is no longer restricted to a landing page -> human signup -> dashboard flow.

A future UberBond commercial surface can support:

agent discovers UberBond -> reads manifest/skill -> requests minimum delegated scopes -> obtains a quote or diagnostic -> creates a bounded workspace/account on behalf of its principal -> purchases or provisions only when authority exists -> receives structured receipts -> returns the result to the principal.

This creates a new GTM lane: **Agent Acquisition Optimization (AAO)** alongside SEO, outbound, partnerships, referrals, marketplaces, and content.

## New money loops

1. Agent-native UberBond products: expose revenue-leak audits, lead-path evidence packets, research, enrichment, reconciliation, and white-label fulfillment through bounded machine interfaces.
2. Agent-native transformation service: sell businesses a productized audit/build that makes their existing SaaS or service operable by AI agents.
3. Agent discoverability audits: verify whether major agent systems can understand, authenticate to, and successfully use a product without UI-only blockers.
4. Machine customer funnel analytics: track discovery -> manifest read -> capability match -> delegated identity -> trial/provision -> paid action -> renewal.
5. Agent-native affiliate/partner surfaces: make partner inventory and offers machine-readable while retaining authorization, attribution, and reconciliation gates.

## Security and economic laws

- Capability does not imply authority.
- Agent identity acts only on behalf of a principal.
- Delegation can only attenuate permissions.
- Destructive operations require a dry-run first.
- Payment or spend cannot exceed explicit delegated bounds.
- Every material action emits a receipt.
- Human UI remains supported; agent-native access is additive.
- External performance or market claims remain unverified until independently evidenced.

## Integration targets

- Gamechanger: detect agent-native market shifts and products.
- Genesis: synthesize new agent-native business models and GTM paths.
- Capability Genome: expose compatible tool/skill bundles to agents.
- Revenue Decision Desk: decide which agent-native opportunities deserve execution.
- Payment truth and reconciliation: bind machine-initiated commercial actions to real cleared economics.
- Founder exception market: surface only decisions that genuinely require owner authority.
- Economic telemetry: compare agent-acquired vs human-acquired contribution profit and founder minutes.

## North Star

Agent-native architecture is valuable only when it improves risk-adjusted cleared contribution profit per founder minute or materially expands UberBond's opportunity set without violating truth, authority, or safety boundaries.
