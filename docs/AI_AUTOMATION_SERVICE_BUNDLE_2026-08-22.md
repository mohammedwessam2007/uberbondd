## Truth classification

Every posted price above is `CREATOR_CLAIM` only. The screenshot is not buyer evidence, transaction evidence, a market-price benchmark, or proof of UberBond revenue. The ten services enter UberBond as `RESEARCH_ONLY` opportunities until independent current buyer evidence and eventually cleared-payment evidence exists.

## Master Two thesis

Do **not** build ten unrelated mini-agencies. Build the shared capability spine once, then compose many offers from it.

The ten offers collapse into five economic families:

1. **Conversation + lead response** — voice receptionist, missed-call text-back, lead follow-up, customer re-engagement, appointment reminders, estimate follow-up.
2. **Reputation operations** — review monitoring, classification, drafted responses, escalation and coverage metrics.
3. **Commercial system of record** — CRM identity, lifecycle state, tasks, attribution, payments/delivery references and audit history.
4. **Decision intelligence** — source adapters, metric contracts, dashboards, freshness/provenance and contribution-profit reporting.
5. **Distribution** — lawful prospect research, provenance, fit scoring, personalized drafts, deliverability/suppression, reply handling and attribution.

The reusable primitives are the real asset: identity/contact graph, CRM state, event triggers, conversation state machines, telephony and messaging adapters, calendars, consent/suppression, templates, model routing, knowledge retrieval, task queues, audit receipts, outcome attribution, dashboards, reliability monitoring and policy/consequence gates.

## Strategic implication

UberBond should become capable of selling and fulfilling many members of this bundle without founder labor scaling linearly. A capability should be promoted only after it passes technical truth, buyer truth, delivery truth, payment truth and repeatability truth. The system may master all ten over time, but it should sequence them by shared capability reuse and economic evidence rather than treating the screenshot's prices as facts.

## Immediate integration rules

- Deduplicate against existing canonical opportunities before creating a new opportunity ID.
- Preserve the screenshot's wording/prices as source claims rather than replacing them with invented market estimates.
- Research current buyer demand and current operator pricing separately.
- Build only reusable primitives that unlock multiple offers.
- External messages, public review posts, calls, CRM writes, booking changes, spend, payments, deployments and production changes remain governed consequences.
- Synthetic success cannot advance an opportunity to `ECONOMICALLY_PROVEN`.
