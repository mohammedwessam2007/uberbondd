## Thesis

The most expensive form of intelligence should be temporary.

A capability begins as flexible frontier reasoning because its structure is unknown. Once repeated experience establishes a stable verified transformation, UberBond should attempt to compile that transformation into progressively cheaper representations, eventually including reconfigurable physical dynamics when justified.

The target is a **cognitive phase transition**:

`frontier reasoning -> reusable trajectory -> verified operator -> deterministic/symbolic law -> compact learned operator -> dynamical law -> physical implementation`

The system should reserve general frontier computation for genuinely novel residue while mastered cognition becomes increasingly cheap, local and sovereign.

## Donor evidence

These donors show that portions of computation can be embodied in physical structure. They do not prove the proposed end-to-end compiler.

- Passive/diffractive optical systems can perform transforms through wave propagation with extremely low active compute in the core; full-system energy still includes illumination, sensing, conversion and control.
- Programmable metasurfaces are being developed as reconfigurable physical AI substrates.
- A 2026 Nature Communications system distributed learning/inference across a computational electromagnetic space and used model-free physical perturbations to adapt the environment directly.
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74554-3
- Reconfigurable photonic logic and photonic neural processors demonstrate that one physical substrate can be reprogrammed for multiple operations.
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75750-x
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71599-2
- Programmable photonic meshes and phase-change/nonvolatile optical weights provide a path toward low-energy reconfiguration rather than fixed one-function optics.
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-025-00831-7
- Physical neural networks and thermodynamic computing motivate direct use of natural dynamics instead of digitally simulating every operation.

## The new mechanism: tiered intelligence crystallization

Every capability has a crystallization state.

### Tier F: Frontier

General model/agent reasoning. Maximum flexibility, maximum marginal compute.

Use only when the problem contains unresolved novelty.

### Tier T: Trajectory

A successful reasoning/tool trajectory is retained with provenance and verification, but it still replays multiple steps.

### Tier O: Operator

Repeated trajectories are compressed into a parameterized reusable operator or wormhole with explicit applicability conditions.

### Tier L: Law

The operator is replaced by deterministic code, a theorem, invariant, constraint rule, symbolic transform or exact solver whenever possible.

### Tier D: Dynamics

A stable family is compiled into a fixed-point, attractor, reservoir, ODE or other dynamical representation in the Lawspace IR.

### Tier M: Matter

High-use, stable, high-amortization dynamics become candidates for realization in a reconfigurable physical substrate such as photonics, analog electronics, memristive networks, FPGA/neuromorphic fabrics or future unconventional compute.

Movement downward requires benchmarked equivalence. Movement upward is always possible when assumptions change.

## The crystallization law

A function should move toward a cheaper tier when:

`expected_future_savings > compile_cost + verification_cost + invalidation_risk + migration_cost`

and only when transfer to held-out inputs is demonstrated.

A frequently used stable operator has high crystallization pressure. A rare or rapidly changing capability remains flexible.

## Decrystallization

Frozen intelligence must not become fossilized error.

Every crystallized object carries:

- provenance;
- dependency hashes;
- applicability domain;
- expiry/freshness conditions;
- known counterexamples;
- reference implementation;
- equivalence tests;
- rollback path;
- authority class.

When dependencies change, the object degrades or returns to a more flexible tier until revalidated.

## Intelligence as a cooling process

The architecture can be viewed as an entropy gradient:

`unknown -> exploratory -> recurrent -> structured -> algorithmic -> physical`

Novel cognition is high-entropy and expensive. Mature cognition is low-entropy and cheap.

This creates an asymmetry unavailable to a stateless general model: UberBond's cost for a stable capability can fall with use while the capability remains available.

## Cognitive JIT compiler

A resident compiler watches expensive repeated work and asks:

1. Is the same causal transformation recurring?
2. Can natural-language reasoning be removed?
3. Can the operation become deterministic code?
4. Can an invariant or theorem replace the search?
5. Can the state update become a fixed-point/dynamical law?
6. Is usage high enough to justify physical specialization?
7. Can the specialized form be verified against the flexible reference on adversarial held-outs?
8. Is rollback immediate if drift appears?

The compiler never grants external authority. It changes execution mechanics only.

## Physical JIT

The most speculative extension is **physical JIT compilation**.

A stable Lawspace program is mapped onto an available physical substrate. The mapping problem becomes hardware synthesis:

`Cognitive Law IR + substrate description -> physical configuration + calibration + verifier`

The same law can target different substrates. No one material becomes UberBond's identity.

Physical execution is accepted only when its output agrees with the reference semantics within the declared tolerance and the full-system energy/latency accounting shows a real advantage.

## Why this could matter for private frontier-effective intelligence

A public foundation model must remain general across essentially every user's unknown future requests.

A single-user exocortex can accumulate an enormous private distribution of recurring structures. If most recurring structures crystallize, then the proportion of requests requiring frontier-scale fresh inference can fall over time.

The desired curve is:

`fraction_frontier_compute(t) -> downward`

while

`verified_capability(t) -> upward`

This is not unlimited computation. It is an attempt to make accumulated intelligence stop costing frontier-scale compute on every invocation.

## Ultimate combination with Lawspace

Lawspace discovers and executes artificial cognitive laws.

Cognitive Matter decides which proven laws deserve to become progressively cheaper execution substrates.

Together:

`novel problem -> frontier reasoning -> semantic constraint extraction -> Lawspace dynamics -> verified solve -> wormhole/law crystallization -> cheaper digital execution -> optional physical embodiment -> near-zero marginal core compute for repeated stable structure`

The system then spends saved compute on the remaining novel frontier.

## The decisive experiment

Do not begin with exotic fabrication.

Build a software-only tiered compiler and measure whether repeated task families migrate from model-heavy reasoning into deterministic/law/dynamics tiers while preserving held-out correctness.

Success criterion:

- verified accuracy is preserved;
- fresh model tokens per task fall;
- wall-clock falls;
- total compute falls including compiler overhead;
- decrystallization catches changed assumptions;
- transfer survives unseen instances.

Only after that should one target a physical backend.

## Truth boundary

- Passive optical cores are not literally zero-energy systems; sources, detectors, conversions, calibration and losses matter.
- A physical accelerator is not automatically more efficient end to end.
- Specialized physics does not evade complexity theory.
- No current evidence shows this stack sustains GPT-6-class general intelligence on commodity hardware.
- The novelty claim is limited: the exact end-to-end synthesis was not found in the public material reviewed, but independent undisclosed or differently named work may exist.
- The research objective is to generate falsifiable evidence, not to rename known photonics or analog computing as a breakthrough.
