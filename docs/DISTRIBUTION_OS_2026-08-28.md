# UberBond Distribution OS — capability reconciliation

## Objective

Build one distribution brain above UberBond's existing channel engines. Do not wholesale-clone external platforms or create competing control planes.

## Current UberBond overlap discovered before implementation

Current `main` already contains a causal attribution spine, partner-fit/account-overlap policy, referral ledger, canonical outreach/deliverability machinery, omnichannel communication, social scheduling, voice, booking, CRM sync, web extraction and economic payment/delivery/renewal truth. Therefore affiliate/referral, attribution, sender orchestration and generic workflow engines are **not** clean-sheet gaps.

## Missing primitive selected

The missing primitive is a **cross-channel economic portfolio allocator**. It must rank eligible distribution candidates using cleared contribution per founder minute, not reach/click/reply/lead vanity metrics.

The channel taxonomy covers cold outreach, partner/referral, affiliate, lifecycle email, SMS/WhatsApp, voice, organic social, content/SEO, community, marketplace, creator partnerships, earned media/PR, paid-media planning, and product-led distribution.

## Non-negotiable laws

1. Trusted economic receipts outrank leading indicators.
2. Attribution must be confirmed; ambiguous attribution earns zero economic credit.
3. One attribution receipt cannot be credited to two channels.
4. Founder minutes are in the denominator.
5. Stale/future/low-confidence economic evidence does not teach allocation.
6. Mixed currencies are not silently summed.
7. Reputation, saturation, complaint, suppression and platform policy can veto a channel.
8. Cold outreach can route only through the canonical outreach engine.
9. Person-targeted channels require reference-only authority/suppression/consent evidence as applicable.
10. Paid media remains plan-only with `spendAuthority: NONE`.
11. Content/SEO plan is not publication truth.
12. No raw recipient/content/secrets in durable portfolio payloads.
13. Exploration is explicit opt-in.
14. Tests make zero provider calls and zero external effects.

## What “world class” means here

It does not mean copying the largest number of tools. It means UberBond can continuously compare every lawful channel family on the same economic denominator, preserve channel-specific safety boundaries, avoid double attribution, down-rank saturation, and reallocate only when trusted receipts justify it. Real top-percentile performance remains an empirical external claim that must be proven with live commercial outcomes.
