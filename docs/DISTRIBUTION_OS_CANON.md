# UberBond Distribution OS Canon

## Purpose

Distribution is a first-class economic allocation problem. UberBond should unify existing lead intelligence, enrichment, outbound, inbound, CRM lifecycle, booking, voice/omnichannel capability contracts, commercial truth and learning behind one provider-neutral control plane.

This document is a capability and integration contract. It does not authorize live sends, calls, provider actions, spend or production mutations.

## North star

Choose the next lawful distribution action that maximizes expected **risk-adjusted cleared contribution profit per founder minute**, subject to authority, consent, suppression, provider policy, budget, capacity, deliverability and evidence constraints.

Volume is a constraint variable, not the objective.

## One machine, not clone soup

Reference products can illuminate surfaces, but UberBond owns the canonical truth and allocator.

| Reference surface | Capability to absorb | UberBond boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apollo | target/persona/filter/search construction | canonical audience evidence + dedupe |
| Clay | enrichment waterfalls | provenance, confidence, freshness, cost ceilings |
| Instantly | campaign/sequencing/mailbox health | outbound safety, suppression, provider receipts |
| 6sense/Common Room | intent/account signals | evidence only until validated; no permission inference |
| Ocean-style lookalikes | similarity expansion | bounded candidate generation, never authority |
| Hunter/ZeroBounce-style hygiene | contact/delivery hygiene | lawful data source, confidence and suppression gates |
| CRM platforms | lifecycle synchronization | UberBond remains canonical commercial ledger |
| marketing automation suites | triggers/segments/journeys | canonical orchestration and consequence gates dominate |
| social/content schedulers | channel execution | provider adapter + platform/authority policy |
| affiliate/referral systems | partner attribution | durable partner/referral identity and payment truth |
| analytics/experimentation | attribution/holdouts | measurement cannot become payment/customer truth |

## Canonical pipeline

`opportunity -> offer -> audience -> evidence/enrichment -> eligibility -> channel candidates -> allocation -> authority/consequence gate -> provider adapter -> provider receipt -> response/inbound -> conversion -> cleared payment -> accepted delivery -> renewal/expansion -> learning`

Every stage is replay-safe and must preserve provenance.

## Distribution action envelope

A future executable action should bind, at minimum:

- stable action/occurrence identity;
- source commit and policy version;
- opportunity and offer version;
- segment/persona/account/contact identity where lawful;
- evidence snapshot digest, freshness and confidence;
- channel and provider adapter identity;
- consent/suppression/authority decision;
- sender/mailbox/domain health snapshot where relevant;
- budget/cost ceiling and capacity reservation;
- experiment/holdout identity;
- expected value, expected cost, founder minutes and downside score;
- allocation reason;
- external-effect ledger;
- provider receipt identity if execution occurs;
- attribution chain identities.

No raw secrets or unnecessary customer payloads belong in the control-plane receipt.

## Channel portfolio

The allocator should be able to reason across, rather than hard-code, channel families:

1. governed cold outbound email;
2. inbound/reply conversion;
3. partner/referral/channel sales;
4. product-led/free-tool distribution;
5. content/SEO and social distribution;
6. communities/directories/marketplaces where terms permit;
7. paid acquisition only when separately authorized and economically bounded;
8. SMS/chat/voice/telephony only with explicit lawful authority and provider adapters;
9. reactivation/retention/renewal/expansion to already-authorized relationships;
10. browser-assisted back-office actions behind consequence gates.

A channel being technically available does not make a recipient/action eligible.

## Allocation score

A practical allocator can start from a transparent score such as:

`expected cleared contribution * evidence confidence * deliverability/feasibility * retention factor - marginal cost - downside penalty`

then divide or otherwise normalize by founder minutes and scarce capacity.

The implementation must expose the components rather than hide them in one magic score. Missing economic evidence lowers confidence or produces `UNKNOWN`; it must not be replaced by fabricated conversion rates.

## Experiments

Every material strategy change should support:
- stable experiment identity;
- treatment/control or holdout where feasible;
- predeclared primary economic metric;
- minimum evidence window/sample policy;
- deliverability/reputation guardrails;
- stop/kill thresholds;
- immutable assignment and replay safety;
- no winner declaration from opens/clicks alone when cleared contribution is the target.

## Attribution

Attribution is a chain of evidence, not a marketing dashboard opinion.

Preferred chain:

`distribution exposure/receipt -> response/inbound evidence -> qualified opportunity -> commercial commitment -> cleared payment -> accepted delivery -> renewal/expansion`

If a link is missing, preserve partial attribution with explicit confidence rather than claiming causal revenue.

## Deliverability and reputation

Outbound allocation must respect canonical suppression and safety. Relevant controls include mailbox/domain health, bounce/complaint evidence, provider limits, warm-up state where supported, sending caps, unsubscribe/opt-out handling, recipient dedupe and kill switches. A high predicted conversion score never overrides a safety gate.

## Self-improvement

Learning may update audience/channel/offer priors only from trusted receipts. Worker prose, synthetic benchmark results, creator screenshots, opens and unverified CRM fields cannot manufacture economic truth.

The system should learn at multiple levels:
- segment/persona fit;
- offer/version fit;
- channel/provider fit;
- timing/cadence;
- copy/creative treatment;
- deliverability risk;
- cost-to-serve and founder-minute burden;
- payment, acceptance, retention and expansion outcomes.

## Capability gap protocol

Before cloning/building another distribution product feature:
1. inspect current main for equivalent capability;
2. identify the missing canonical primitive or adapter;
3. research current public/official references and license/terms where code reuse is considered;
4. implement the smallest provider-neutral gap;
5. keep live effects disabled by default;
6. hostile-test authority, suppression, replay, provenance and contradictory truth;
7. merge only with appropriate evidence.

## Completion meaning

"Distribution OS complete" can only mean the internal control plane and required adapters/tests for a declared scope are complete. It cannot mean 100% of all future channels, providers or market knowledge, and it cannot mean real-world distribution success without external receipts. The frontier is expected to evolve as channels, providers, laws and markets change.
