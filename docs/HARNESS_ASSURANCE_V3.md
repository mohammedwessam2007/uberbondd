## Source-architecture verdict

**10 / 10 dimensions represented on main after PRs #864 and #865.**

This is the completion of the *internal architecture objective* from this donor wave. The objective was not to copy the article. It was to metabolize its strongest primitive and combine it with UberBond's deeper machinery so the resulting harness is stricter than the donor design.

## Why this is materially stronger than the donor

The donor supplied the insight that one frontier reasoning substrate can impersonate a multi-disciplinary engineering institution if roles and phases are explicit.

UberBond adds the missing constitutional skeleton:

`CONTEXT -> ROLE -> SPEC -> ARCHITECTURE -> PLAN -> TEST -> BUILD -> INDEPENDENT REVIEW -> PROOF -> RISK -> AUTHORITY -> EXECUTION -> MONITORING -> REALITY RECONCILIATION -> LEARNING -> REVOCATION`

The important mutation is that **intelligence, evidence, and authority are separate currencies**.

A model can become more capable without receiving more power. A passing benchmark cannot manufacture external truth. A green internal test cannot become a customer acceptance receipt. A risk layer can narrow or stop action but cannot widen the lease. A self-improvement candidate can propose promotion but cannot promote itself. An intended effect loses instantly to a contradictory observed effect.

## Remaining evidence outside this 10/10 rubric

These are deliberately excluded from the source-architecture score because software cannot honestly close them by itself:

- profitable live trading or market alpha;
- cleared revenue;
- customer acceptance and retention;
- provider acceptance and real provider failover where not yet observed;
- legal clearance where applicable;
- long-duration unattended operation;
- longitudinal life outcomes;
- literal ASI / superintelligence evidence.

Those stay external-proof questions and must never be converted into architecture percentages.

## Permanent rule

**A powerful model is raw cognitive energy. UberBond's job is to turn that energy into a proof-carrying institution without allowing capability to impersonate authority or simulation to impersonate reality.**
