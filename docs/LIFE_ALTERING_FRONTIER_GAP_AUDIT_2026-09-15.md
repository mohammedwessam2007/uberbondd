## Clean-room donor synthesis

This patch reproduces useful *capabilities and workflow patterns*, not proprietary source, branding, secrets or protected assets.

### Adaptive mastery

- ALEKS / Knowledge Space Theory: represent feasible learner knowledge states and diagnose efficiently rather than assuming a linear syllabus.
- Carnegie Learning MATHia: action-sensitive tutoring and continuous learner feedback.
- Anki FSRS: explicit desired-retention scheduling rather than one fixed review cadence.
- UberBond extension: prerequisite repair, uncertainty-ranked diagnostics, Human Capability Genome compatibility, transparent state transitions and evidence-relative mastery.

References: https://www.aleks.com/about_aleks/knowledge_space_theory ; https://www.carnegielearning.com/solutions/math/mathia ; https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options

### Relationship sovereignty

- Monica: open-source personal-relationship management with private/self-hostable contact context.
- UberBond extension: network-bridge scoring, capability-gap-to-mentor matching, reciprocity/trust signals, provenance and an explicit law that relationship intelligence never grants messaging authority.

Reference: https://github.com/monicahq/monica

### Geographic sovereignty

- European Commission Regulated Professions Database: profession-by-country authority records and competent-authority links.
- ECFMG/Intealth Pathways: seasonal, expiring professional-mobility requirements and certification evidence.
- UberBond extension: source authority classes, checked-at timestamps, maximum evidence ages, stale-evidence penalties, dependency blockers and configurable option-value comparison.

References: https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/regprof/ ; https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/

### Model foundry

- Hugging Face PEFT/TRL: parameter-efficient post-training patterns such as LoRA/QLoRA.
- NVIDIA Megatron Core: composable distributed large-model training primitives and custom-model infrastructure.
- vLLM: high-throughput serving target.
- UberBond extension: provider-neutral dataset license/consent gates, recipe hashes, checkpoint lineage, eval-completeness and regression gates, budget/GPU-hour caps, rollback identity and the invariant `DEPLOYABLE_CANDIDATE != DEPLOYED`.

References: https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/en/peft_integration ; https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/index ; https://docs.nvidia.com/megatron-core/developer-guide/latest/ ; https://docs.vllm.ai/

## Truth invariants

- No training data with known secrets or consent violations.
- Unknown or stale mobility evidence remains unknown or stale.
- A relationship recommendation never authorizes contact.
- A model checkpoint never becomes production merely because it exists.
- No external effect is authorized by any module in this patch.
- Human goals such as languages, medicine, fitness, wilderness competence, acting, negotiation and family support route through existing capability/experience/project systems rather than creating redundant software organs.
- Real-world outcomes remain external evidence. Software cannot certify that a skill was learned, a visa is available, a relationship exists, a model was trained, a company earned money or an institution exists without receipts.
