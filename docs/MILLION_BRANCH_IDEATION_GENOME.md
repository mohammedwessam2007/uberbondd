This organ extends, and does not replace, the 275-idea Perpetual Frontier registry. The 275 named ideas remain no-amputation historical/canonical search-space memory. This genome adds a deterministic generative tensor of **20 UberBond domains × 50 idea-generating operators = 1,000 meta-generators**.

The purpose is not to warehouse one thousand ordinary ideas. Each generator is a reusable question-producing mechanism that can create tens or hundreds of candidate branches, crossbreed them across domains, turn failures into new generators, and convert successful mechanisms into reusable capability atoms.

## Core law

`1,000 META-GENERATORS -> MANY CANDIDATES -> EVIDENCE/FEASIBILITY TOURNAMENT -> CROSS-DOMAIN RECOMBINATION -> NEW GENERATORS -> REPEAT`

The founder's current imagination, the present ontology, and this 1,000-generator seed are all floors rather than ceilings. The terminal meta-question is:

> **What would a stronger UberBond ask that current UberBond is not intelligent enough to ask yet?**

## Public/private isolation law

This public genome is deliberately founder-generic. It may define personalization mechanisms, but it must not encode the founder's identity, private biography, education path, specialty preference, relationships, device/location traces, health facts, or other private-life state. Personalization enters only through a separately authorized private runtime/context boundary. Capability to ingest private context never creates authority to publish, persist, transmit, or act on that context.

## Recursive expansion protocol

For every activated generator:

1. Generate 20–100 candidate ideas.
2. Score novelty, expected value, feasibility, founder-specific fit from authorized private context, reversibility, evidence quality, time-to-test, and cross-domain leverage.
3. Keep the strongest candidates while preserving weird low-probability branches as dormant search-space memory.
4. Crossbreed survivors with at least three other domains.
5. Turn surprising failures into new generator hypotheses.
6. Turn successful mechanisms into reusable capability atoms.
7. Re-run dormant generators when technology, capital, people, data, or life context changes.
8. Invent additional operators and domains whenever the current ontology cannot express reality without distortion.

## Integration contract

The canonical executable implementation is `src/million-branch-ideation-genome.mjs`.

`buildMillionBranchIdeationGenome()` deterministically materializes all 1,000 generators from the compact 20×50 genome.

`buildIdeationActivationPlan()` selects a bounded deterministic subset for a specific GENESIS context rather than flooding every cycle with all one thousand prompts.

The live Perpetual Frontier GENESIS tick activates this organ for every valid frontier cycle, while `npm run genesis:doctor` validates both the historical 275-idea registry and this 1,000-generator extension.

The system may project large search spaces arithmetically, but projected branches are not executed ideas, discoveries, capabilities, customers, revenue, or reality evidence.

## Truth and authority boundary

This organ generates internal hypotheses and research directions. It creates no customer, provider, deployment, spend, physical action, external communication, scientific proof, or life-outcome proof. Existing authority and consequence gates remain separate.

The goal is **open-ended imagination with disciplined reality selection**: millions of possibilities in the brain, few evidence-selected bets in the world.
