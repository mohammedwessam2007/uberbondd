# UberBond October 2026 Flagship Offer Quartet — 10/10 Design Revision
Date: 2026-09-28

Status: design-complete against the current offer-quality rubric. Prices remain market hypotheses until paid acceptance exists. No offer is treated as commercially validated merely because it scores 10/10 internally.

## Rubric
Each offer must satisfy:
1. painful current problem;
2. obvious economic buyer;
3. observable/evidenced failure;
4. fixed bounded scope;
5. useful pre-sale evidence;
6. async purchase/delivery path;
7. simple acceptance criterion;
8. credible price/value relationship;
9. reusable fulfillment;
10. natural expansion/recurring path.

## Offer 1 — Agency Revenue Leak Proof Pack
Replaces: White-Label Lead-to-Booking Leak Audit.

Buyer:
Owner / Head of Client Services / Performance Director at agencies serving HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing and adjacent home-service businesses.

Pain:
Agency generates leads but cannot prove whether client-side capture, booking, phone handling or follow-up is leaking value. This creates renewal friction because the agency can be blamed for downstream conversion failure.

Promise:
"In 72 hours, get a white-label evidence pack showing where three client lead paths break or become unverifiable, with screenshots, severity, and repair order."

Scope:
- exactly 3 client sites;
- 1 highest-value lead journey per site;
- mobile + desktop;
- form, booking, call/tap, confirmation and publicly observable handoff checks;
- no unauthorized form submission or internal-system access;
- one retest of documented defects after repair.

Deliverables:
- agency-branded one-page summary per client;
- evidence screenshots;
- failure taxonomy;
- severity and confidence;
- repair-order backlog;
- "proven / probable / unknown" claim boundary;
- reusable client-renewal slide.

Price hypothesis:
- $1,500 fixed for 3 client sites;
- $3,900 for 10 sites;
- $2,500/month monitoring hypothesis after successful delivery.

Risk reversal:
If fewer than three material, reproducible findings survive the agreed evidence threshold across the three sites, expand the benchmark/review at no extra charge or offer refund/credit according to payment policy.

Async path:
Cold evidence → "want the map?" → artifact → fixed scope → payment → URLs/access checklist → delivery → approve/revise. No meeting required.

Acceptance:
All promised journeys tested; evidence reproducible; no unsupported lost-revenue claim; white-label pack usable by agency.

Why stronger:
Sells agency retention/proof, not merely a website audit. The unit of value is client-account protection.

## Offer 2 — AI Agent Production Release Gate
Replaces: AI Agent Release Gate / Production Acceptance Sprint.

Buyer:
AI agencies, MSPs, internal AI platform teams, SaaS teams shipping agents or tool-using workflows.

Pain:
Agent demos work, but teams lack trustworthy release evidence across tool use, edge cases, policy boundaries and repeatability.

Promise:
"Before the agent ships, get a reproducible PASS / CONDITIONAL PASS / FAIL release packet against your real workflow."

Scope:
- one authorized production-like workflow;
- 40–60 representative evaluation cases;
- deterministic checks where possible;
- human/model-judge separation;
- tool-call correctness;
- refusal/policy boundaries where relevant;
- 10+ adversarial/edge cases;
- repeatability runs;
- failure taxonomy;
- one post-fix retest.

Deliverables:
- executable/portable test corpus;
- failure matrix with evidence;
- release verdict;
- known-limitations statement;
- prioritized fix order;
- retest receipt;
- management summary.

Price hypothesis:
- $3,000 fixed standard;
- $4,000 complex/tool-heavy workflow;
- $3,500/month continuous release-gate hypothesis;
- larger multi-agent portfolio only after paid proof.

Async path:
Sample failure/eval observation → fixed scope → secure access checklist → payment → run → release packet → retest. Meeting optional.

Acceptance:
Reproducible run; agreed case count; every verdict evidence-linked; failure taxonomy and limitation statement accepted.

Why stronger:
Sells a release decision and evidence packet, not generic "AI testing."

## Offer 3 — Revenue Proof & Renewal Pack
Replaces: Client ROI Proof Sprint / Renewal-Ready ROI Evidence Pack.

Buyer:
Performance agencies, paid-media agencies, RevOps partners and fractional CMOs with a client renewal or budget-defense problem.

Pain:
Client asks "did this create revenue?" while ad platforms, analytics, CRM and payment records disagree. Agency cannot safely claim causality from mismatched dashboards.

Promise:
"Turn the client's messy marketing-to-revenue trail into a renewal-ready evidence pack that separates what is proven, probable and unknown."

Scope:
- one client;
- up to 3 authorized source systems;
- campaign / lead / CRM / revenue reconciliation;
- discrepancy map;
- attribution-definition comparison;
- evidence trail;
- no invented incremental-revenue claim;
- one clarification/retest cycle.

Deliverables:
- executive one-pager;
- reconciliation table;
- proven/probable/unknown ledger;
- source-definition conflicts;
- renewal/budget-defense narrative constrained to evidence;
- next instrumentation fixes;
- client-ready appendix.

Price hypothesis:
- $2,500 fixed;
- $4,500 for 3-client agency pack;
- $3,000/month evidence/reporting hypothesis after repeat demand.

Async path:
Show one discrepancy → "want the proof map?" → scope/data checklist → payment → secure ingest → pack → agency review → final client-ready version.

Acceptance:
All stated claims trace to evidence; discrepancies reproducible; no unsupported recovered-revenue or incrementality claim.

Why stronger:
2026 agencies are explicitly under pressure to connect marketing to revenue. This turns reporting pain into a fixed deliverable with claim discipline.

## Offer 4 — GCC Arabic-English Booking Parity & Revenue Leak Sprint
Replaces: Arabic + English Booking Leak Audit / Bilingual Appointment Recovery Sprint.

Buyer:
GCC clinic/medspa groups and agencies serving them, initially Saudi/UAE, with Arabic + English web/booking journeys.

Pain:
Arabic and English versions can diverge across mobile layout, CTA visibility, forms, WhatsApp/call links, booking steps, error states, confirmation and tracking, creating invisible conversion/experience inconsistencies.

Promise:
"Get a side-by-side Arabic/English booking-journey evidence pack showing every material parity break and booking obstacle, with screenshots and repair order."

Scope:
- one location / one primary booking journey;
- Arabic + English;
- mobile-first + desktop confirmation;
- RTL layout;
- CTA visibility;
- call / WhatsApp / booking links;
- form-field parity;
- validation/error/confirmation parity;
- consent/privacy copy presence where observable;
- one post-fix retest;
- no clinical/medical-quality judgment.

Deliverables:
- bilingual journey diff;
- paired screenshots;
- severity/confidence;
- repair order;
- language/UX parity matrix;
- client-ready bilingual summary.

Price hypothesis:
- $1,750 single location;
- $4,500 three-location pack;
- $3,500/month multi-location monitoring hypothesis after paid proof.

Async path:
Send one paired Arabic/English finding → offer evidence map → fixed scope → payment → delivery → retest. Meeting optional.

Acceptance:
Every reported parity break reproducible; Arabic/English evidence paired; no unsupported clinical/revenue claim.

Why stronger:
Narrow buyer + narrow journey + measurable bilingual parity turns "Arabic QA" into a commercial booking-assurance product.

## 10/10 design invariant

A 10/10 internal design score means the offer has no obvious structural weakness against the current rubric.

It does NOT mean:
- proven demand;
- proven price acceptance;
- guaranteed close rate;
- proven contribution margin;
- verified recurring demand.

The first paid transaction can falsify the score. Real buyer evidence outranks the rubric.
