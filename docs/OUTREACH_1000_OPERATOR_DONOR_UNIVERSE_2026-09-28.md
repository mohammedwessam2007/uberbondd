# UberBond 1,000-Operator Cold-Outreach Donor Universe — 2026-09-28

Status: research universe, NOT a claim that a verified global "top 1,000" leaderboard exists.

## Why this exists

No common audited dataset ranks 1,000 people by cold-email performance. Public evidence mixes platform telemetry, vendor case studies, practitioner doctrine, community rankings, marketplace reviews and social influence. UberBond therefore treats "top 1,000" as a donor-discovery universe and scores strategy atoms source-by-source instead of manufacturing a prestige ranking.

## Evidence-weighting rule

For each operator/strategy claim preserve:
1. direct cold-email evidence;
2. denominator and sample size;
3. total reply vs positive reply vs meeting vs revenue;
4. whether results repeat across campaigns/clients;
5. current recency;
6. strategy specificity and reproducibility;
7. independent vs vendor/self-reported evidence;
8. conflicts and contradictory outcomes.

Prefer large observed datasets and repeated outcome evidence over follower counts.

## Highest-value current donors

### Jason Bay + Gong + 30MPC
Strategy atoms: buyer-language, short/concise copy, priority/problem framing, relevant proof, offer/interest CTA instead of immediate meeting ask, systematic follow-up.
Why: Gong/30MPC report analyses of 28M+/85M+ cold emails. Their CTA report associates meeting asks with -44% reply impact, asking about the problem with -29%, asking for interest with +7%, and making an offer with +28%. Observational, not randomized causal proof.

## Strategy convergence

Across the evidence-rich operators, the recurring atoms collapse to a much smaller genome:
1. Narrow ICP and problem ownership.
2. Fresh reason-now / trigger.
3. Prefer direct observable problem evidence when possible.
4. Relevance before decorative personalization.
5. Deep 1:1 research only when account value justifies it.
6. Short, plain, low-cognitive-load copy.
7. Buyer language, not seller jargon.
8. Describe consequence and ideal state rather than product features.
9. Relevant proof / trust.
10. Useful artifact, benchmark, teardown, diagnostic or other offer before demanding calendar time.
11. Interest/permission CTA.
12. Follow-ups add new information, not "just bumping."
13. Test offer/angle/message components independently.
14. Optimize qualified positive reply, meeting, opportunity and cleared revenue, not opens.
15. Scale volume only after message-market fit and sender health survive.

## UberBond record-mode synthesis

The strongest current candidate architecture is:

verified observation about the exact account
-> consequence the recipient owns
-> small piece of proof / credibility
-> pre-built useful artifact
-> permission CTA

For the White-Label Lead-to-Booking Leak Audit, the first email should usually sell the evidence artifact, not UberBond and not a meeting.
