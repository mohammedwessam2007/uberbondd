# Live activation checklist

The founder button is source-ready only when each runtime fact below is already true and observable:

- both canonical outreach roots are owned and intended for outreach;
- at least one real sending mailbox is materialized and authenticated;
- mailbox warm-up/health supplies a positive bounded daily cap;
- DNS/authentication evidence is fresh;
- a legitimate observed egress/transport route is ready;
- recipient-provider budget evidence is positive;
- suppression and unsubscribe state are available;
- exact recipient contact route and recipient-specific legal eligibility are evidenced before dispatch;
- at least one campaign is approved and `autoSend=true`;
- `OUTBOUND_ENABLED=true`;
- `OUTBOUND_DRY_RUN=false`;
- zero uncertain outbound effects require reconciliation.

If any item is missing, the button must remain a refusal surface rather than manufacturing readiness.
