# UberBond Sovereign Salience Router

Status: **FOUNDER_SPEC promoted into bounded attention-routing source foundation**

The Sovereign Salience Router composes with `Gamechanger for Life` and the `Life Sensorium`. It does not decide whether a signal is relevant to Mohamed's life. Existing world/life relevance and attention-budget machinery performs that filter first. This organ answers the next question:

> **Given something relevant, how should it reach Mohamed's attention right now?**

The answer space is deliberately small:

- `INTERRUPT_NOW`
- `PREPARE_SILENTLY`
- `DEFER`
- `PRESERVE_MYSTERY`
- `SAY_NOTHING`

The target is not maximum notification throughput. It is **maximum useful intelligence with minimum unnecessary occupation of Mohamed's consciousness**.

## Core law

`SENSING MAY SCALE -> REASONING MAY SCALE -> PREPARATION MAY SCALE -> INTERRUPTION MUST REMAIN SCARCE`

A mature ambient UberBond should often feel quieter as it becomes more capable.

## Founder-declared salience rules

The executable router recognizes:

- `RIGHT_NOT_TO_KNOW`
- `NO_SPOILERS`
- `DIRECT_EXPERIENCE_FIRST`
- `DO_NOT_SURFACE`
- `EMERGENCY_ONLY`
- `NORMAL`

Founder-declared non-surfacing dominates heuristic importance. More information never grants permission to expose itself.

## Interruption threshold

An immediate interruption must earn its attention cost. The router considers urgency, importance, evidence confidence, cost of interruption, reversibility of delay, direct-experience value, and consequence if missed.

Importance alone is not urgency. Confidence alone is not consequence. A reversible high-value item can often be prepared silently rather than interrupting.

Batch routing also caps how many items may interrupt or occupy prepared-attention slots. This prevents a thousand-agent intelligence system from becoming a thousand-agent notification system.

## Mystery and direct experience

Some experiences lose value when prematurely explained or predicted. `NO_SPOILERS` and `DIRECT_EXPERIENCE_FIRST` therefore route to `PRESERVE_MYSTERY`. High direct-experience value may also trigger a heuristic mystery-preservation posture when the consequence of silence is low.

Any heuristic preservation can be overridden by Mohamed's present choice.

## Authority boundary

Attention routing is not external-action authority. `INTERRUPT_NOW` means UberBond may surface information through an already-authorized interface. It does not authorize customer contact, spending, deployment, physical action, credential change, medical intervention, or conversion of a recommendation into Mohamed's choice.

`PREPARE_SILENTLY` likewise means prepare only within already-authorized private/internal boundaries.

The router is a source mechanism, not evidence that real ambient sensing, private delivery hardware, or longitudinal intervention value currently exists.
