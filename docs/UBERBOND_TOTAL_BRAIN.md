## 0. The no-amputation law

A new offer, branch, architecture, model, agent, mission name, or implementation **does not erase** older UberBond goals.

Superseded implementation != deleted goal.

Every future UberBond session must preserve useful ideas, capabilities, constraints, experiments, product families, opportunity research, mechanism research, named initiatives, architecture donors, unresolved names, and chat-only goals until they are deliberately reconciled.

Silence is not deletion. A capability not mentioned in the current handoff may still belong to the long-horizon company genome.

### Truth classes

Use these labels instead of flattening everything into one fake status:

- `VERIFIED_CURRENT`: supported by current repository code/exact-head receipts or durable current external evidence.
- `DRAFT_BRANCH`: exists on a current/recent branch, draft PR, isolated worktree, issue, or unmerged execution lane.
- `HISTORICAL_DONOR`: superseded architecture/implementation whose useful mechanisms or goals still survive.
- `CHAT_SPEC_GOAL`: explicitly requested or designed in project conversations but not proven current implementation.
- `RESEARCH_ASSET`: market/company/mechanism/opportunity/vendor/provider research or generated candidate space that is useful input but not business proof.
- `EXTERNAL_BLOCKED`: internally prepared but dependent on provider, account, customer, payment, legal, credential, reputation, or elapsed-time evidence.
- `SUPERSEDED_TRUTH`: a formerly canonical claim explicitly replaced by stronger later evidence.
- `OWNER_RECALLED_UNRESOLVED`: a remembered name/goal that must remain discoverable until source evidence resolves it.

Current repository/external receipts always outrank this superset memory for present-tense claims.
