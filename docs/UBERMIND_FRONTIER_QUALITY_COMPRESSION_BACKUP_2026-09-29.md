## 7. The real $10 research target

The objective is **not** "run cheap AI for a long time."

It is:

> **Preserve frontier-level consequential reasoning while making the marginal cost of autonomous operation collapse.**

Illustrative engineering bands preserved from the chat frontier:

- ~300–600 autonomous hours / $10: dense frontier intervention.
- ~1,000–2,000 hours / $10: strongly orchestrated frontier-governed execution.
- ~5,000 hours / $10: aggressive engineering target using sparse frontier decisions plus broad downstream execution.
- ~10,000–30,000 hours / $10: extreme research target for a highly crystallized event-driven system.
- ~50,000+ hours / $10: upper-envelope scenario if frontier decision boundaries become very sparse.
- literal wall-clock lifetime is unbounded if the system can remain idle/event-driven without inference.

These are **architecture scenarios, not observed UberBond results**.

The deeper metric is:

	extbf{reality-changing work correctly governed / frontier thought}
