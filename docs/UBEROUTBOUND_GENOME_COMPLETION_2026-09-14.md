## 100,000/day doctrine

`100,000/day` is a **capacity horizon, never a quota**.

Runtime utilization remains bounded by the minimum of:

`qualified eligible inventory, legal eligibility, verified contactability, healthy sender/domain/egress capacity, recipient-provider budget, experiment/risk policy, and separate consequence authority`.

The quality floor may never be lowered merely to fill infrastructure capacity.

## Causal doctrine

Observational Gong, Lavender and practitioner findings are priors. They are not promoted to universal truths by repository text.

Promotion path:

`PRIOR -> RANDOMIZED TEST -> HIERARCHICAL/CAUSAL ANALYSIS -> HOLDOUT VALIDATION -> BOUNDED PROMOTION -> CONTINUOUS MONITORING -> REVOKE/REPLACE IF DEGRADED`

Contextual bandits belong after causal baselines, not before them.

The most important confound is explicitly represented:

`TRIGGER SELECTION EFFECT != TRIGGER MENTION EFFECT`

A prospect can be selected because a signal predicts buying propensity while a separate randomized treatment determines whether mentioning that signal improves the message.

## Economic doctrine

The terminal outbound objective is not open rate, raw reply rate or send volume.

The long-horizon target is:

`incremental cleared contribution profit subject to quality, reputation, compliance and authority`

with the mature marginal decision concept:

`MarginalSendValue = ExpectedProfit - ExpectedReputationDamage - ExpectedComplianceRisk - ExpectedOpportunityCost`

Missing economic terms remain unknown rather than becoming zero.
