# UBERREPLY 4x25K Portfolio Canon

Date: 2026-09-15

Status: source-level commercial/outbound strategy extension. This document does not claim live demand, live 100K/day capacity, any specific reply rate, or revenue.

## Founder decision

UberBond's 100,000/day outreach objective is diversified across four commercial pain classes rather than one offer:

1. `LEAD_TO_BOOKING_LEAK_AUDIT` — target 25,000/day.
2. `AI_AGENT_RELEASE_GATE` — target 25,000/day.
3. `CLIENT_ROI_PROOF_SPRINT` — target 25,000/day.
4. `BILINGUAL_BOOKING_LEAK_AUDIT` — target 25,000/day.

The split is a target, not a quota that permits bad recipients. If a lane has only 8,000 high-quality legally eligible recipients, UberBond does not silently steal 17,000 from another lane. Cross-lane spillover requires a separately governed decision.

## Offer A — White-Label Lead-to-Booking Leak Audit

Buyer: agencies serving HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and adjacent local-service businesses.

Economic problem: an agency can generate enquiries while remaining unable to prove where those enquiries fail to become booked jobs. The buyer needs defensible downstream evidence before the contractor blames acquisition.

Promise: trace one client lead path and produce partner-brandable evidence showing where the path breaks, stalls, disappears, or loses attribution, plus a prioritized repair backlog.

Initial paid canary remains the existing `$450` fixed-scope experiment. That number is a willingness-to-pay hypothesis until external payment evidence exists.

Expansion candidates: portfolio audits, Monthly Lead-Path Assurance, white-label agency retainer.

## Offer B — AI Agent Release Gate

Buyer: AI implementation agencies, SaaS teams, and agent builders.

Economic problem: customer-facing agents can pass happy paths but still fail procedures, final states, escalation logic, or unauthorized-action constraints after release.

Promise: independently exercise a versioned agent against buyer-approved failure/final-state scenarios and return acceptance evidence, traces, failures, and a repair backlog.

Initial paid canary inherits the prior `$900` AI Agent Acceptance Evidence Sprint hypothesis.

Expansion candidates: release acceptance, recurring regression gate, portfolio/agency agent assurance.

## Offer C — Client ROI Proof Sprint

Buyer: performance, PPC, SEO, growth, and lead-generation agencies.

Economic problem: platform activity is frequently easier to report than downstream calls, forms, CRM states, buyer-origin revenue, and contribution. Weak reconciliation increases client skepticism and churn pressure.

Promise: reconcile marketing activity to downstream evidence, preserve contradictions and missing links, and return client-ready proof plus the unresolved evidence map.

Initial price is a new hypothesis, not established willingness to pay.

Expansion candidates: Monthly Revenue Proof, Client Retention Assurance, portfolio reconciliation.

## Offer D — Arabic + English Booking Leak Audit

Buyer: UAE/KSA clinics, medspas, dental groups, and other high-LTV appointment businesses with bilingual journeys.

Economic problem: acquisition and booking may cross ad, web, WhatsApp, phone, language, staff, and scheduling boundaries. The buyer needs evidence of where Arabic/English enquiries lose continuity before a booked appointment.

Promise: trace the authorized bilingual booking journey and return reproducible handoff evidence plus a repair backlog.

Initial price is a hypothesis, not established willingness to pay.

Expansion candidates: multi-location audit, Booking Assurance Monitor, channel/language portfolio assurance.

## UBERREPLY message genotype

Canonical first-touch structure:

`SIGNAL -> TENSION -> INSIGHT -> GIFT -> TINY_ASK`

Donated principles are preserved, not treated as universal causal truth:

- Show-Me-You-Know-Me style contextual relevance.
- Josh Braun style problem illumination / resistance reduction.
- Gong / 30MPC problem-before-product and short-message priors.
- Jason Bay offer/asset CTA and problem-linked signal doctrine.
- New-information follow-ups rather than empty bumps.
- Personalization must be economically causal. Decorative biography is not sufficient.

First-touch starting constraints:

- subject: 1–4 words;
- one primary ask;
- no default calendar/meeting ask;
- preferred first conversion: permission to send an already-prepared micro-artifact;
- evidence-backed signal required;
- never claim an artifact exists before it exists;
- problem before product;
- low-pressure plain language;
- research depth S/A/B allocated by account value, signal strength, evidence density, artifact feasibility, and research cost.

These are priors. Segment-specific experiments may supersede them when causal/down-funnel evidence earns that change.

## Micro-campaign law

`25,000/day` does not mean one 25,000-person template campaign.

Each lane decomposes into cells:

`offer -> vertical -> persona -> trigger -> problem hypothesis -> evidence type -> research depth -> genotype -> CTA -> sequence policy`

Current external benchmark research supports treating large undifferentiated campaigns as a risk. Woodpecker's current benchmark surface reports materially higher median replies for small campaigns than campaigns above 10,000 recipients, and lower per-mailbox volume is also associated with better reply rates. This is observational platform evidence, not a guarantee for UberBond.

Research donor references used for the extension include:

- Gong / 30MPC 85M+ cold-email analysis: https://www.30mpc.com/newsletter/the-data-backed-cold-email-formula-the-exact-words-length
- Woodpecker current benchmark surface: https://woodpecker.co/cold-email-benchmarks/
- Woodpecker 2026 statistics synthesis: https://woodpecker.co/blog/cold-email-statistics/
- Instantly 2026 benchmark: https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026

Practitioner donors such as Samantha McKenna and Josh Braun remain hypothesis donors unless UberBond's own randomized/down-funnel evidence validates the donated strategy in a segment.

## Experiment law

UBERREPLY does not declare one hybrid frozen forever. It runs persistent, content-addressed experiments across dimensions including:

- `SEND_ASSET` vs `INTEREST` CTA;
- tension question vs tension statement;
- trigger mention vs trigger selection without mention;
- account-signal vs problem-noun subject;
- one-page artifact vs three-finding artifact;
- three-touch default vs evidence-conditioned extension up to six touches.

Persistent holdouts and canonical experiment assignment are inherited from UberOutbound Genome.

The preferred terminal experiment metric is:

`INCREMENTAL_CLEARED_CONTRIBUTION_PROFIT`

Raw opens and raw replies cannot promote a strategy.

## Tournament / economic Darwinism

A strategy's ranking hierarchy is:

1. guardrail safety;
2. cleared contribution per 1,000 provider-confirmed sends;
3. paid sprint rate;
4. accepted-delivery rate;
5. qualified-conversation rate;
6. qualified-positive-reply rate.

Therefore a low-reply strategy may correctly beat a high-reply strategy when it produces better buyers and more cleared contribution.

The tournament reuses `economic-feedback-allocator.mjs` for bounded internal weight proposals after enough paid/accepted outcomes exist. Exploration floors remain for non-revoked strategies.

A strategy can be revoked from next-cycle selection when canonical promotion/degradation evidence establishes harm, legal/provider incidents, reputation ceilings, or negative causal/economic evidence. Revocation changes internal selection weight only. It creates no external-effect authority.

## Offer-level kill / rethink law

For the existing first-cash doctrine, five qualified buyer conversations with zero paid pilot produces `RETHINK_OFFER`, not "send more until something happens."

Repeated paid + accepted + positive-contribution evidence can produce `SCALE_REVIEW_CANDIDATE`.

Price changes, increased spend, expanded authority, or cross-lane reallocation remain separately governed.

## Truth boundary

Source implementation can establish that the portfolio, strategy priors, experiment assignment, economic weighting, promotion, degradation, and revocation logic exist.

It cannot establish:

- that any of the four offers is externally 10/10;
- a future reply rate;
- willingness to pay;
- live 100,000/day sender capacity;
- inbox placement;
- cleared revenue;
- accepted delivery;
- retention or expansion.

Those become facts only through external receipts.
