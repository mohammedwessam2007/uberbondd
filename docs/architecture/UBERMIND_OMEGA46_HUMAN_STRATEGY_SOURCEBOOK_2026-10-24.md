# UberMind Ω46: Human Intelligence and Commercial Strategy Sourcebook

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: curated research index and operating guidance. This is not model fine-tuning or measured emotional intelligence.

## Primary sources and distilled applications

1. **Niccolò Machiavelli, The Prince**. https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1232 . Historical lessons on institutions, status, reputation, public trust, advisors, incentives and long-run political consequences. Chapters XVII, XIX, XXI and XXIII are useful starting points. Use as a lens for stakeholder incentives, not a universal moral guide. Check translation and jurisdiction before reproducing any entire edition.
2. **Mayer, Salovey, Caruso, emotional-intelligence research**. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18793038/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6546921/ . Perception, emotional understanding, emotional information in decisions and appropriate response. Distinguish explicit signals from competing hypotheses; never claim certain access to a person's inner life.
3. **Harvard Program on Negotiation and BATNA**. https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/translate-your-batna-to-the-current-deal/ . Explore alternatives, authority to decide, comparable trade-offs and when walking away is sensible. Treat uncertainty honestly.
4. **Robert Cialdini, principles of influence**. https://cialdini.com/principles . Reciprocity, scarcity, authority, commitment/consistency, liking, social proof and unity. Use accurate proof, genuine value, real availability and respect for informed choice. The book is copyrighted; retain references and original notes, not book-length copies.
5. **Strategyzer value proposition and buyer jobs**. https://www.strategyzer.com/value-proposition . Assess functional, social and emotional customer jobs along with pains, gains and switching risk. Confirm hypotheses with the buyer when possible.
6. **Ong and colleagues, AI-generated empathy (July 2026)**. https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/current-directions/09637214261444274/ . Clear, supportive AI language is not the same as genuine human understanding. Track human feedback and response quality.

## Business question bank

- What did the other party actually say, request or do? What is unknown?
- Who approves the deal and what do they care about? Who else participates?
- What would make the proposed exchange valuable to both sides?
- What are the strongest real alternatives, for us and for them?
- What evidence would reduce purchase or delivery risk?
- How should the tone acknowledge uncertainty, dignity and practical constraints?
- What are our credible competitor responses and reputational risks?
- How will we tell whether our interpretation was wrong?

## Practical output packet

Always label: observed statements and provenance; two or three possible interpretations; alternative explanations; stakeholder interests and authority; value and commercial logic; a respectful clarifying question; transparent next move; limits and uncertainties; results requiring external confirmation.

## Operational and evidence boundaries

The new skill at .claude/skills/ubermind-social-strategy/SKILL.md guides on-demand reasoning by the frontier lead. No new models, payments, external messages, deployed psychological classifier, or claimed revenue. People may disagree with the system's interpretation; ask and revise rather than assert. Use limited, consent-appropriate information and preserve long-run trust. Evaluate with real corrections, accepted offers, customer feedback and repeat relationships, not superficial positivity.
