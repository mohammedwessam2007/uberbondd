## The one metric the founder cares about

A complex task consumes 100% of a five-hour Claude Pro allowance in ordinary Claude. The first STRETCH TARGET is for UberMind to consume **30% or less** while meeting the **same independently accepted quality** on the identical task and source state. That is 70% target reduction, 3.333x theoretical comparable-task capacity if repeatable. **None of that has been measured on the account yet.** For largely novel problems, real necessary Opus effort can remain near 100%; never lower quality to hit a number.

The fixed existing cash bill is $20 monthly under included Claude Pro usage, not an amount refunded by tokens. Extra JEV/OpenRouter, Claude API, Fable and other models are NOT included and remain disabled without existing explicit authorization. Neither 83.5 billion Haiku cached-input projections nor Anthropic API list-price equivalence set a private five-hour session quota.
