## Additional bound: cumulative hostile-page network traffic (next commit)

The previous 96-route cap is insufficient alone: up to 96 public HTTP responses may otherwise saturate sockets and bytes before the limit trips. `src/inquiry-path-runtime-witness.mjs` now admits at most **8 simultaneous** pinned-public GET route handlers and at most **24,000,000 total response bytes** across the witness; exceeding either sets the global `requestBudgetExceeded` flag, aborts additional requests, and prevents any subsequent page from being certified. The preexisting page route counter (96) remains in force. `tests/commercialization-browser-adversarial.test.mjs` injects bounded response/fanout conditions through a test-only `fetchPinned` seam while the non-injectable production `assertPublicUrl` guard remains active. `tests/browser.test.mjs` adds a *real Chromium* malicious-page fixture with 140 resources and attempted POST, using a synthetic response provider and **no outbound HTTP**. The test is not a verified real-browser success until the exact-head hosted browser job passes. This is resource-safety hardening, not proof that untrusted browser code is risk-free.
