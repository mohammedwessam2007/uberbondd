# A02: opt-in evidence protocol, NO CONTACT AUTHORISATION

This is a read-only preparation packet. The owner cannot infer permission to reach a buyer or submit to Upwork from these notes. No credentials, spam or off-platform contact allowed.

For a future explicitly authorized, active platform posting: verify that exact listing's current detail, its requirements and permitted application route; retain public URL and UTC capture. Verify the account can lawfully apply and pay/receive in the founder's jurisdiction; stop otherwise. A truthful proposal may ask only about one gap, existing analytics availability, site access boundaries, intended audit outcome and acceptance criterion. No invented cases/results or unverified credentials. Do not use people's email addresses, identities or private business data.

Record distinct evidence tiers: **POSTED_REQUEST** (historical search summary), **AUTHENTICATED_DETAIL** (live platform), **AUTHORIZED_APPLICATION**, **CUSTOMER_RESPONSE**, **SCOPE_ACCEPTED**, **DELIVERY_ACCEPTED**, **SETTLED_PROVIDER_AMOUNT**, **BANK_CLEARED_NET**. Reject any skipped stage. A rejection, stale job, no reply or platform denial leaves customer count and money at zero.

Only after real buyer acceptance, founder-approved scope, data permissions, legal/invoice clearance and exact payment authority may fulfilment or billing move. No one-click submit or autonomous browsing to apply is authorized here. Human-only steps remain separate and cannot be faked.
