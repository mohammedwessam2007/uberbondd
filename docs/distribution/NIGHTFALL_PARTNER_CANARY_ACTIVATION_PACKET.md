Convert UberBond's current partner-first distribution decision into an executable preparation path without contacting anyone, inventing demand, or treating a directory listing as permission.

Current canary hypothesis:

**White-label Lead-Path Revenue Leak Evidence Sprint** for agencies serving HVAC, plumbing and electrical businesses.

Current price hypothesis: **USD 450 fixed scope**. This is unvalidated and must remain fixed during the first bounded canary unless a new version is deliberately created.

This packet does not activate outreach, checkout, provider calls or customer work.

## Why partner-first remains the highest-leverage preparation motion

Current public ecosystem research shows multiple existing aggregation surfaces around home-service software. ServiceTitan maintains a Certified Provider program and explicitly describes Certified Marketers as trade-focused marketing professionals. Jobber maintains a partner directory including a Digital Marketing category and separately describes its partner program as serving people and companies who work directly with home-service businesses. HighLevel maintains Certified Admin and Developer Partner directories. Google maintains a Partner directory for advertising agencies and third parties.

These are valuable because a qualified agency may represent multiple downstream contractor accounts. They are **target-evidence surfaces**, not proof that any listed company wants UberBond.

## Candidate qualification state machine

`SOURCE_SEED -> PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_RESEARCH -> VERTICAL_FIT_VERIFIED -> MULTI_CLIENT_OPERATION_VERIFIED -> OFFER_FIT_VERIFIED -> CONTACT_PROVENANCE_PENDING -> COMPLIANCE_PENDING -> SENDER_READINESS_PENDING -> OWNER_AUTHORITY_PENDING -> ELIGIBLE_FOR_BOUNDED_CANARY`

A candidate may stop at any state.

### SOURCE_SEED

Requires one current public ecosystem source.

This proves only that the source lists or classifies the company.

### PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_RESEARCH

Use public business websites and legitimate public sources only.

Collect references, not copied private data.

### VERTICAL_FIT_VERIFIED

Require current public evidence that the agency serves HVAC, plumbing, electrical or a closely adjacent trade category.

Do not infer vertical fit from vague phrases such as "local businesses".

### MULTI_CLIENT_OPERATION_VERIFIED

Require at least three public client examples, case studies, portfolio entries, or equivalent evidence that the company operates as an agency across multiple downstream accounts.

This gate exists because the economic thesis is one-to-many leverage.

### OFFER_FIT_VERIFIED

Require evidence that the agency works on at least one relevant surface such as websites, lead generation, paid search, conversion, booking, CRM/revenue operations, or customer journey optimization.

Exclude direct conflicts where the proposed evidence sprint would merely duplicate a tightly bundled incumbent service with no independent-value angle.

### CONTACT_PROVENANCE_PENDING

Do not collect or infer private contact information during qualification.

If the candidate later reaches this state, use only a publicly presented business contact route under the canonical provenance rules.

### COMPLIANCE_PENDING

Before any contact, determine the applicable jurisdiction, commercial-message rules, provider terms, suppression/complaint state, required sender identification, unsubscribe mechanism and physical-address requirements.

A business directory entry is not consent.

### SENDER_READINESS_PENDING

Require current domain/mailbox/provider readiness, sender health, SPF/DKIM/DMARC where applicable, current caps, suppression, idempotent send reservation, uncertainty reconciliation and kill controls.

### OWNER_AUTHORITY_PENDING

No live contact occurs until the founder grants consequence-specific authority for the exact bounded campaign, audience and channel.

### ELIGIBLE_FOR_BOUNDED_CANARY

Only then may a record enter the first outreach sample.

## First bounded commercial sample

The current research doctrine remains:

- maximum **five qualified conversations** before price/offer review;
- no paid pilot after five qualified conversations triggers a commercial kill/rethink;
- do not expand volume simply to avoid learning from rejection;
- track founder minutes from research through qualification and conversation preparation;
- track every transition from source to eventual payment/acceptance if activation occurs.

The number five is a canary discipline, not a claim about statistical significance.

## Outreach message preparation contract

When external gates are eventually cleared, messages should be composed from evidence rather than generic personalization.

The preparation packet may reference:

1. the public ecosystem signal;
2. a verified home-service specialization signal;
3. a specific public client/lead-path pattern worth independently checking;
4. the exact bounded white-label evidence sprint;
5. the explicit fact that UberBond is offering evidence and reproducibility, not a revenue guarantee.

Never state that the agency is losing revenue unless buyer-origin evidence proves it.

Never state that UberBond recovered revenue before external payment/delivery evidence exists.

## Product-led supporting motion

The second-ranked distribution motion remains a read-only lead-path diagnostic/proof artifact.

Its preparation can continue without messaging, but publication or testing against a buyer-controlled surface remains separately gated.

The diagnostic should return observed facts such as:

- missing/ambiguous service-area information;
- visible hours/FAQ/contact-path configuration;
- reproducible public-path friction;
- evidence references;
- scope limitations.

It must not output invented dollar-loss estimates.

## Attribution envelope

Every future activated candidate must link:

`source -> candidate -> qualification evidence -> channel/motion -> campaign -> offer version -> touch -> response -> qualified conversation -> payment occurrence -> delivery receipt -> customer acceptance -> renewal/expansion -> variable cost -> founder minutes`

Without that chain, a channel may produce activity but cannot win economic allocation.
