## Decision

UberBond should not sell generic cold outreach and should not activate a high-volume sender fleet.

The first distribution portfolio is:

1. **Partner / white-label:** agencies serving HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies.
2. **Product-led evidence:** one safe lead-path diagnostic or checklist that terminates in the paid evidence sprint.
3. **Evidence-led direct outbound:** only after jurisdiction, provenance, suppression, provider policy, DNS, mailbox, sender-health, budget, and owner-authority gates pass.
4. **Owned evidence content:** buyer questions and original diagnostic explanations, not scaled commodity pages.

The offer is the `White-label Lead-Path Revenue Leak Evidence Sprint`. The distribution system must sell a bounded evidence outcome, not a CRM, dashboard, sequencer, or bundle of commodity automations.

The exact machine portfolio is [distribution-channel-portfolio.json](../../artifacts/distribution-channel-portfolio.json). Capacity and cost boundaries are in [outreach-capacity-economics.json](../../artifacts/outreach-capacity-economics.json).

## Why this portfolio

Jobber's 2026 seller survey says more than 55% of surveyed customers expected a response within an hour and names communication and quote workflows as material. Housecall Pro's 2026 seller survey of more than 1,100 US homeowners reports repair-led demand, predictable trigger moments, and a directional link between speed and emergency-job selection. These are useful directional signals, not independent proof of UberBond demand or price acceptance. Sources: [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/) and [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/home-service-spending-report/).

The same market is commoditized at the feature layer. Current field-service and CRM incumbents already provide response, scheduling, quote, reminder, review, and follow-up features. Therefore the differentiator must be cross-system evidence, reproducibility, recovery, acceptance, and economic lineage. A generic automation bundle is rejected.

An agency is both a buyer candidate and a leverage node: one relationship can expose several downstream sites. The sprint is partner-brandable, can be bounded to one downstream lead path, and does not require UberBond to replace the agency's or client's system of record.

## Partner system

### Initial partner definition

An evidence-qualified partner candidate should have:

- a public agency or consultancy identity;
- explicit HVAC, plumbing, electrical, home-services, field-service, or local-service specialization;
- at least three public downstream examples or another verifiable leverage indicator;
- a public business contact route whose provenance can be recorded;
- no suppression, complaint, or policy conflict;
- a plausible reason to value independent, partner-brandable evidence rather than another CRM.

Directory membership, a case-study logo, or a public email is not demand, consent, authority, or qualification by itself.

### Partner economic envelope

Before a partner motion can scale, record:

- downstream accounts made reachable;
- wholesale price, partner margin, and discount;
- onboarding and support minutes;
- provider and delivery variable costs;
- time to cleared payment;
- delivery acceptance and repair burden;
- renewal and adjacent-site expansion;
- channel concentration and platform dependence.

Partner program fees are not justified today. For example, HubSpot's 2026 program publicly describes a paid membership and directory/revenue-share mechanics, while Duda demonstrates a white-label multi-client/API model. These are mechanism references, not recommendations to buy. Sources: [HubSpot 2026 partner program](https://www.hubspot.com/solutions-partners-tiers-and-benefits-2026) and [Duda white label](https://www.duda.co/website-builder/white-label).

The earlier repository ceiling of `$15/month` for two mailboxes is not backed by a public quote. Maildoso's current public minimum is `$75/month` for 30 mailboxes and publishes a 15-cold-email daily mailbox cap; a custom two-mailbox price is not public. This contradiction blocks procurement, rather than becoming a convenient assumption. Source: [Maildoso pricing](https://maildoso.ai/pricing).

### Capacity law

The smallest proposed canary is two mailboxes, one per verified domain, capped at ten cold emails per mailbox per business day. That is a proposed UberBond ceiling, not a provider guarantee. It remains disabled until exact provider cost and every upstream gate are real.

The public Maildoso minimum would create nominal capacity for 450 daily cold emails, and 100 mailboxes at its published 15-per-day cap would create nominal capacity for 1,500 daily emails. Neither is a commercial target. Volume must not be purchased before a smaller probe demonstrates cleared payment, accepted delivery, positive contribution, and safe sender health.

### Jurisdiction and provider boundary

This is operational research, not legal advice.

- United States commercial email still requires accurate identity/subject information, a valid postal address, and a functioning opt-out under the [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).
- UK guidance distinguishes corporate subscribers from sole traders/partnerships while preserving identity, opt-out, and personal-data duties. See the [ICO B2B marketing guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/).
- Canada remains disabled without qualified consent evidence under the [official CASL guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email).
- Australia remains disabled without qualified consent and compliant unsubscribe handling under [ACMA guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam).
- EU/member-state and all other routes require exact jurisdiction adjudication; no global inference is allowed.

Gmail's provider requirements include authentication and bulk-sender DMARC/unsubscribe controls, and it says spam rates should remain below 0.3%. Yahoo publishes a comparable complaint ceiling. UberBond's current complaint circuit breaker is stricter at 0.1%. Sources: [Google sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126) and [Yahoo best practices](https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/).

### Outbound event contract

Every eventual recipient-level event must bind:

`origin signal -> account/person or audience -> public-contact provenance -> jurisdiction -> channel -> motion -> campaign -> offer version -> touch -> response -> qualification -> payment -> delivery -> acceptance -> renewal/expansion -> cost -> founder minutes`

No private email inference. No bought list. No suppression bypass. No generic model personalization. A verification result proves likely deliverability, not consent or provider permission.

### Kill and pause thresholds

Pause before any next send when:

- public-contact provenance or jurisdiction is unresolved;
- suppression, complaint, or opt-out state is uncertain;
- DNS, authentication, provider health, or sender health is blocked;
- a provider result is uncertain;
- the daily cap or authorized budget is exceeded;
- the current source-code bounce threshold or stricter complaint threshold is crossed;
- the bounded sample produces no qualified conversation by its declared end.

Commercially, opens and replies do not keep a weak experiment alive. The canary continues only when downstream evidence moves toward a cleared payment and accepted delivery.

## Product-led distribution

The first asset should not be a generic ROI calculator. Without buyer-origin transactions, a “revenue leak” dollar amount would be fabricated.

An eligible diagnostic should instead return:

- exact public path observed;
- timestamp and evidence hash;
- reproducible fact or `UNVERIFIABLE`;
- severity and why it matters;
- explicit untested surfaces;
- safe next action;
- versioned invitation to the fixed evidence sprint.

The asset must not submit a form, call a number, send a message, log into a system, or infer a revenue figure without exact authority and evidence.

## Owned distribution

Each owned asset should answer one buyer question and terminate in useful proof.

Good initial question classes include:

- “How can an agency prove a home-service lead path is working without replacing the CRM?”
- “What evidence should a lead-response audit preserve?”
- “What can and cannot be inferred from a public booking or quote flow?”
- “How should an agency test a client lead path without contacting real customers?”

Google's current guidance says normal SEO practices still apply to AI search features, emphasizes original useful content, and does not require special AI markup. Its spam policy prohibits scaled content abuse. OpenAI documents `OAI-SearchBot` controls for ChatGPT search discovery. Sources: [Google AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), and [OpenAI bots](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).

Programmatic SEO is therefore deferred until UberBond has a real evidence corpus whose pages would each have distinct buyer value. AEO is a distribution and measurement adaptation, not permission to mass-produce prose.

## Marketplace, API, and MCP distribution

Shopify and Zapier demonstrate that app directories can produce embedded discovery and partner support, while the official MCP Registry provides a discovery surface for agent tools. These are future routes, not current build priorities. Sources: [Shopify App Store economics](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share), [Zapier partner program](https://zapier.com/developer-platform/partner-program), and [official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/).

The first eligible embedded surface is a read-only, permission-scoped evidence diagnostic after repeated paid utility. A generic write-capable “UberBond company MCP” is explicitly rejected.
