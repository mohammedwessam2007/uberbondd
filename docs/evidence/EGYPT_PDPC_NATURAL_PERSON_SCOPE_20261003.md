# Current PDPC scope guidance

Authority: REGULATOR_GUIDANCE. https://portal.pdpc.gov.eg/faq
Retrieved and displayed 2026-10-03, not substituted with an older search-index snapshot.

The actor definitions describe a data subject as a natural person to whom electronically processed personal data relates, legally or factually, enabling distinction from others. Personal-data categories concern identified or identifiable natural persons, including indirect identification through other data.

The current EDM answer frames regulation around lawful processing of personal data and individuals' rights. Consent is from the data subject; identification and withdrawal requirements remain. Authorisation is required where applicable. The FAQ does not establish an independent universal corporate-contact license rule solely because an operator is Egyptian.

Interpretation: consistent with statutory natural-person material scope, not affirmative regulator-specific approval of an individual company. Territorial nexus is assessed separately. A company-role label alone is insufficient: explicit company-only review, absence of identified/targeted natural persons and enrichment, verified first-party scoped invitation and fresh statutory evidence are required. Any personal-data ambiguity remains held.
