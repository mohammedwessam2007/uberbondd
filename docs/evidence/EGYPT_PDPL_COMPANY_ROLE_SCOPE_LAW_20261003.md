# Law 151/2020 material-scope evidence

Authority: LAW. Official MCIT Gazette scan, retrieved 2026-10-03.
https://mcit.gov.eg/Upcont/Documents/Reports%20and%20Documents_1232021000_Law_No_151_2020_Personal_Data_Protection.pdf

Visual review: promulgating Article 1 (PDF page 2) limits the instrument to electronically processed personal data concerning natural persons. Attached Article 1 (page 4) defines personal data by its relation to an identified or identifiable natural person, and the data subject as that natural person. Chapter 8 Article 17 (pages 16-17) governs direct electronic marketing to the data subject; Article 18 retains consent/contact-data duties referring to that subject.

Promulgating Article 2 separately supplies Egyptian-actor criminal jurisdiction. It does not remove Article 1's material elements. Foreign nationality alone is not an exemption.

Reviewed inference, not a regulator ruling: a verified foreign corporation/function-only invited contact using no natural-person recipient data lacks those material elements. This does not exempt employee contact, personal enrichment, indirectly identifiable people, Egypt recipients, or ambiguous facts. Consent/license duties tied to PDPL personal-data marketing are not applicable to this narrowly reviewed fact pattern. Other law, provider requirements, identity, opt-out, suppression and exact effect authorization remain enforced.

No claimed quotation or verification of an unavailable Executive Regulation download. The inference rests on the controlling statutory scope, corroborated by current PDPC guidance; contrary authoritative scope evidence must revoke it.
