## Invoicing and tax readiness — factual guidance, not a declaration
ETA says freelancers/ecommerce fall under existing income/VAT laws, including the optional Law 6/2025 system for participants. Foreign payment does not itself prove exemption.
ETA's current FAQ states B2B e-invoicing requirements for taxpayers, including electronic signature, service codes and integration. Its export-service guide requires a written scope/contract, electronic invoice with supplier/customer details, and payment evidence. A PayPal invoice alone is not established as an ETA-compliant electronic invoice.
The normal VAT threshold is EGP 500,000, with exceptions including exporters. Do not assume small foreign sales avoid registration. Qualifying remote exported services can be zero-rated; zero-rated VAT is not exemption from income tax or filing.
