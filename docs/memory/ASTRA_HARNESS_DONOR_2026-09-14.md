The article's stronger claims about one-person hedge-fund replacement, extreme cost compression, production equivalence, self-improvement, and market alpha remain unverified by the supplied paper. They must not become UberBond truth merely because the engineering doctrine was useful.

## UberBond truth boundary

- market alpha;
- cleared profit;
- customer acceptance;
- provider acceptance;
- legal clearance;
- real-world trading performance;
- life outcomes;
- ASI or superintelligence evidence.

Those require their own admissible observations.
