# UberBond No-Amputation Law — 2026-09-05

Status: PERMANENT PROJECT LAW

UberBond closure must never be achieved by silently deleting useful capability, mechanism, evidence, history, donor implementation, test, idea, branch lineage, Frontier work, or repository memory.

Rules:

1. Repair before removal.
2. Reconcile before replacement.
3. A stronger implementation may supersede an older implementation, but the older mechanism and evidence must remain recoverable and its useful behavior must not disappear without an explicit, evidence-backed revocation reason.
4. Prefer additive wrappers, compatibility facades, supersession markers, archival references, reversible deactivation, quarantine, or gated reachability over destructive simplification.
5. Perpetual Frontier never stops. Optional new capabilities may continue in parallel and do not move the fixed Core Completion finish line merely by existing.
6. Historical receipts and failed-run evidence are evidence, not clutter. Preserve them.
7. Temporary verification-only scaffolding may be removed only when it contains no unique capability or evidence and its result has already been durably preserved elsewhere.
8. Security repairs must preserve legitimate functionality while eliminating unsafe authority or secret exposure.
9. Payment repairs must preserve provider donors and historical truth while strengthening canonical payment evidence.
10. Tests may prove a guard by mutation/fault injection, but production capability must not be amputated merely to obtain a green result.

Core Completion remains a fixed evidence bar: zero material internally-solvable P0/P1/P2 in the required operating core, exact-main proof green, canon current, and remaining gates genuinely external/customer/provider/legal/hardware/elapsed-time.

Perpetual Frontier remains an endless growth lane after Core Completion.
