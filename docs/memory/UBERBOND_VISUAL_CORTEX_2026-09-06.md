# UberBond Visual Cortex Checkpoint — 2026-09-06

## Owner intent

UberBond must expose its full power through a living graph interface that can organize an arbitrarily large organism without amputating hidden, dormant, superseded, historical or currently irrelevant features.

## Canonical law

The canonical Ultimate Graph is the organism self-map. The visual command center is only a projection over it.

**HIDDEN_IN_PROJECTION_NEVER_MEANS_DELETED_FROM_CANONICAL_GRAPH.**

Filtering, clustering, zooming, lens selection, search and focus have zero deletion authority. Every bounded projection reports canonical node/edge counts and hidden counts.

- exact canonical graph digest returned with projections;
- summary, lens/search and bounded two-hop neighborhood modes;
- BRAIN, SOFTWARE, MEMORY, CAPABILITY, ECONOMIC, EVOLUTION and ALL lenses;
- touch/pinch pan and zoom for iPad;
- animated canvas graph with signal particles;
- node selection and relationship drawer;

## Evolution rule

Future historical timeline playback must use real durable graph snapshots or source-history evidence. Current-state nodes may not be assigned invented historical timestamps merely to create a cinematic animation.

Superseded and historical donor nodes remain queryable lineage instead of being silently deleted.

## Authority boundary

The visual cortex has no repository-write, merge, deploy, payment, customer, DNS, credential, spend, messaging or production authority. It is a read-only owner visualization of structural evidence.
