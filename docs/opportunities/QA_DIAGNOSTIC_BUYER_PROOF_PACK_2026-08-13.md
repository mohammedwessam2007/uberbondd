## Demonstration report summary

The fictional report uses a fictional “Northstar Health Studio” website and is marked at the top and bottom as illustrative only. It demonstrates:

- an executive snapshot with P0–P3 counts;
- a test frame with pages, journeys, viewports, and exclusions;
- reproducible finding records;
- expected-versus-observed behavior;
- repair guidance and retest suggestions;
- a prioritized developer queue;
- evidence and limitation language.

Its constructed findings include a mobile-navigation focus issue, a form-confirmation issue, a narrow-screen CTA issue, an inconsistent card target, and a breadcrumb-spacing issue. These are examples of report structure, not observations about a real site.
