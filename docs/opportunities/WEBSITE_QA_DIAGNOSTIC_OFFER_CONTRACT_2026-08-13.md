# Website QA & Release-Readiness Diagnostic — offer contract

Status: `FROZEN_OWNER_REVIEW_ONLY`  
Offer ID: `offer-website-qa-release-readiness-diagnostic-usd250-v1`  
Price: **USD 250 fixed**  
Delivery: **12–24 hours after cleared payment, URLs, and priority journeys are confirmed**

This is the one active commercial experiment. It is deliberately narrow: sell and deliver one paid website QA diagnostic before adding recurring monitoring, a broad platform offer, or another commercial lane.

The machine-readable source is [`website-qa-diagnostic-offer.json`](../../data/opportunity-factory/website-qa-diagnostic-offer.json). This document explains the buyer-facing contract in plain language.

## Buyer and trigger

The intended buyer is a small digital agency, website owner, or delivery lead who is approaching launch or client handoff and wants an independent check of visible public-site journeys.

The trigger should be concrete:

- a launch or client handoff is near;
- a responsive website journey needs a second pair of eyes;
- a team needs a prioritized, developer-ready repair queue.

## What the buyer receives

For the fixed USD 250 fee, UberBond reviews up to three public, unauthenticated websites and the priority journeys agreed at intake. The delivery includes:

- desktop and mobile responsive review;
- navigation, buttons, forms, visible user-flow, and release-readiness checks;
- reproduction steps and expected-versus-observed behavior;
- severity and prioritization;
- minimally necessary redacted screenshots or evidence references;
- an HTML or PDF report and a clean repair queue.

The buyer supplies the URLs and priority journeys before work begins. New websites, private areas, extra journeys, implementation, and retesting are outside the fixed scope unless separately approved.

## Evidence standard

Every real finding must identify the URL or named environment, UTC observation time, viewport/browser context, preconditions, numbered reproduction steps, expected and observed behavior, severity rationale, and appropriate redacted evidence. A report may say that an observable behavior was found; it may not turn that observation into an unsupported conversion, revenue, security, uptime, or compliance claim.

The demonstration report is explicitly fictional and illustrative. It is not customer work, a case study, a certification, or evidence of paid-client results.

## Delivery and acceptance

Work starts only after all three conditions are satisfied:

1. the USD 250 payment is cleared;
2. the agreed public URLs are received;
3. the priority journeys and test boundaries are confirmed.

Delivery is due within 12–24 hours after those conditions are satisfied. The buyer has two business days to accept the report or identify a factual omission against the locked scope. One factual correction is included for an omitted in-scope observation or inaccurate report field. A correction is not a new test pass, a new website, implementation, a retest, or a request to change an evidence-based conclusion.

Acceptance does not mean the website is defect-free, accessible, secure, compliant, high-converting, or revenue-producing.

## Payment, refund, and scope boundaries

Payment is 100% before fulfilment. No testing or report preparation begins on an unpaid or uncleared order. Before work starts, the owner may approve a full refund if the buyer cancels. After work starts, there is no automatic refund because a buyer disputes a finding or expected a different finding count; a pro-rata remedy may be approved only if UberBond cannot deliver the locked scope because of its own failure.

Refunds, credits, disputes, and payment changes are manual owner decisions recorded against a receipt. They are never automatic effects of this document.

The diagnostic excludes credentials, admin panels, private data, staging systems, production databases, purchases, payments, destructive actions, and real form submissions unless explicit written authorization and a safe test identity are provided. It also excludes penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, uptime monitoring, load testing, SEO, analytics, legal/compliance advice, medical advice, accessibility certification, custom PHP/WordPress implementation, and any guarantee of conversion, revenue, security, uptime, or absence of defects.

## Kill conditions

Pause or decline the engagement when:

- payment, B2B ability, or owner approval is unresolved;
- URLs or priority journeys are missing;
- the buyer asks for unapproved private or production access;
- permission to test or the evidence boundary is ambiguous;
- the timebox cannot be met without silently reducing scope;
- the buyer requests fabricated outcomes, unsupported claims, or guarantees;
- delivery effort, correction burden, dispute rate, or payment friction makes the experiment unsound.

After the first paid diagnostic, pause expansion and review delivery quality, acceptance, payment, effort, and commercial fit. The result of that review—not optimism or platform completeness—determines whether the offer is kept, revised, or killed.
