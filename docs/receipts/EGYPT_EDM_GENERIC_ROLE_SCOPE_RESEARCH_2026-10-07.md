## Primary/current Egypt evidence

The Egyptian Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC) currently states that:

- the PDPL regulates electronic personal data belonging to Egyptian citizens, whether inside or outside Egypt, and non-Egyptian citizens residing in Egypt;
- a **Data Subject** is a natural person to whom electronically processed personal data refers and which enables that person to be distinguished from others;
- electronic direct marketing rules require prior consent from the **data subject**, sender/originator identification, contact details, marketing-purpose disclosure, opt-out, records, and the applicable EDM licence/permit.

Current PDPC source:
- https://www.pdpc.gov.eg/

An English translation of Law 151/2020 published in September 2026 states in the issuing Article 1 that the law applies to Personal Data relating to **natural persons** processed electronically, and Article 17 prohibits direct-marketing electronic communication to a **Data Subject** unless its conditions are met.

Current translation source:
- https://www.mondaq.com/data-protection/1832892/egypts-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl-english-translation

Current 2026 Egyptian practitioner guidance also describes territorial reach in nationality/residence terms and preserves the licensing regime:
- https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/egypt
