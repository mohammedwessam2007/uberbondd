## Buyer-facing draft once a buyer genuinely requests scope
**Agency:** [buyer legal entity, authorized decision maker and verified contact route]  
**Project:** Three-client Lead-Path Evidence and Acceptance Pack  
**Price:** **USD $1,500 fixed for exactly three client websites**, subject to written buyer acceptance and final invoicing/payment terms; this is an offer hypothesis, not an accepted price.  
**Target turnaround:** **72 hours after** complete URL/access prerequisites, mutually accepted scope and agreed payment/onboarding trigger. The clock does not start on unsolicited research alone.

**What is included:** On three separate authorized client websites, audit one mutually agreed high-value lead route on mobile and desktop. Map observable click-to-call, booking, form, acknowledgement and handoff expectations. Test only public/nontransactional flows or client-authorized staging/test identities; never impersonate a customer, submit a real service request, or access a CRM without owner-granted credentials and scope.

**Deliverables:** one agency-branded summary per site; evidence-linked before/after or observed/expected screenshots with UTC timestamp and device; failure category, reproducibility steps, severity, confidence; prioritized repair backlog; evidence ledger classifying each material claim **PROVEN / PROBABLE / UNKNOWN**; one client-renewal-ready slide; **one retest** of a documented defect after a client-authorized fix.

**What is not promised:** no fixed amount of additional revenue, no guaranteed booked jobs/positive replies, no claim that existing tracking is broken, no invasive testing or private CRM access by default, no hidden implementation work, no unsolicited form/phone transactions, and no attribution of private backend behavior based solely on public landing pages.

**Evidence threshold:** An actionable defect requires a reproduced, attributable discrepancy with exact path, tested device/state, expected behavior grounded in the buyer's specification, observed behavior and supporting screenshots/URL/time. If no material reproducible defect exists, report a successful/unknown QA result truthfully rather than inventing a leak. **Canonical risk reversal applies:** if fewer than three material, reproducible findings survive the agreed threshold, expand the benchmark/review at no extra charge or offer credit/refund under the **separately agreed payment policy**. Neither refund terms nor a payment instrument are auto-created by this template.

**Acceptance:** The buyer agrees that all three scoped journeys were examined, the report documents reproducible evidence and uncertainty, the pack is white-label usable, and the permitted retest has been supplied where applicable. Buyer may request corrections to report accuracy within an agreed written acceptance window; do not invent a legal acceptance deadline. Final approval is an explicit message from the named buyer, not inferred from silence or a document view.

**Exclusions/changes:** Extra websites/journeys, paid ads attribution, server logs, CRM reconciliation, performance remediation, production fixes and additional retest cycles are separately scoped and approved. All access belongs to the customer and must be least-privilege, revocable, private and contract-bound.
