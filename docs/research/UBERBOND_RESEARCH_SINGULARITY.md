## Research operating system

The research loop is now:

`decision -> decomposed question -> source strategy -> observation -> source class -> contradiction -> confidence/freshness -> opportunity or mechanism update -> decision packet -> monitor only if change can alter the decision`

The signal taxonomy deliberately excludes generic AI news, engagement metrics, competitor feature volume, creator revenue claims, and historical opportunity counts. Those facts consume attention without necessarily changing allocation.

Priority signal classes are:

1. active buyer pain;
2. buyer willingness-to-pay evidence;
3. channel contribution and founder minutes;
4. provider price, limit, policy, or availability;
5. legal/regulatory changes in active jurisdictions;
6. competitor mechanisms that change a specific capability gap;
7. software license/maintenance changes;
8. partner/ecosystem openings;
9. commercial failures and lost deals;
10. repeated founder-minute bottlenecks.

## Commercial falsification

The strongest negative finding is that obvious automations are cheap and crowded. Established software already provides missed-call response, reminders, quote follow-up, reviews, reactivation, sequencing, CRM, and dashboards.

Therefore UberBond cannot justify a price with “AI automation” or software access alone. It must earn value from:

- independent evidence;
- cross-system reconciliation;
- reproducible failure and recovery paths;
- scoped delivery and acceptance;
- partner leverage;
- economic lineage through cleared payment and founder minutes.

This finding rejects a generic backend CRM, generic dashboard, broad AI worker bundle, or ten standalone mini-SaaS offers.

## Opportunity decision

The canary wins because current home-service seller surveys show urgent response/estimate workflows, while an agency can unlock several downstream accounts. The evidence sprint is narrow enough to deliver without owning the client's operational stack.

The canary does **not** yet have:

- a willing buyer;
- validated `$450` price acceptance;
- measured acquisition cost;
- measured delivery or support minutes;
- positive contribution;
- renewal evidence.

The `$450` one-time price is a falsifiable hypothesis, held constant for a bounded probe. It is not a market price or current revenue.

The fallback exists because current agent vendors emphasize simulations, versioning, diagnostics, traces, and realistic evaluation. Sierra's vendor research also reports that current frontier agents still fail many realistic knowledge-heavy tool tasks. That supports the problem mechanism, not the existence of a paying UberBond buyer.

## Distribution decision

Partner / white-label is the primary architecture. Product-led evidence and owned content support it. Evidence-led direct outbound can accelerate learning only after infrastructure and consequence gates pass.

Referral/expansion becomes the likely best channel after accepted customers exist. It is currently blocked because there are none.

Paid acquisition, marketplace distribution, and public API/MCP productization are deferred until contribution and repeated utility exist.

Every future distribution event must preserve:

`signal -> account/audience -> channel -> motion -> campaign -> offer -> touch -> response -> qualification -> payment -> delivery -> acceptance -> renewal/expansion -> variable cost -> founder minutes`

## Outbound economic truth

The existing two-domain activation idea contains a material cost contradiction. The repository's `$15/month` two-mailbox ceiling has no current public quote. Maildoso's published minimum is `$75/month` for 30 mailboxes; the custom exact-quantity price remains external.

Capacity is not value:

- two proposed canary mailboxes at ten cold emails/day each would create 20/day nominal capacity, but exact cost and authorization are unknown;
- Maildoso's public minimum creates up to 450/day nominal capacity at its stated cap, but overprovisions the first experiment;
- 1,500/day requires 100 mailboxes at that cap and is explicitly rejected before measured contribution and sender health.

No expected revenue, reply rate, conversion rate, or break-even count is supplied because the required terms are unknown. The artifact records formulas and hostile cases instead of invented forecasts.
