## Microcapital arithmetic: useful boundaries

- Solana's base fee is `0.000005 × SOL/USD` dollars per signature. A two-transaction round trip starts at `0.00001 × SOL/USD`, before priority fees, DEX/launchpad fees, slippage, token-account funding, failed attempts or fiat access. This formula avoids inventing a current token price. Cheap base fees do not by themselves rule out $1, and do not prove a positive edge.
- Aave's documented initialization premium on $1,000 borrowed is $0.50; on $100,000 it is $50. These are **arithmetic illustrations**, not current pool quotes. A percentage spread must clear that premium, every swap fee, price impact and transaction/inclusion costs. Flash borrowing cannot pay those costs in advance and is not zero-risk capital.
- On a Polymarket crypto market with the displayed rate 0.07, buying two shares at 0.50 costs $1 before a $0.035 taker fee. That illustrative order may also fail the market's own minimum. A full complementary basket must buy equal YES and NO units; its minimum cash can therefore exceed $1 even when a single low-price outcome is affordable.
- IBKR's stated fractional-share rule gives $0.01 commission on $1 value, before the other frictions. Do not misapply the whole-share $0.35 minimum and claim all $1 stock trades are impossible. Funding a foreign brokerage, whole-share tender eligibility and producing $100 profit are separate unsolved steps.

## Position C: executable-contradiction observatory

Why retain this one: bounded binary payoffs permit explicit mathematical falsification without forecasting news. Public order books make the gap between an attractive displayed price and executable size measurable. This is **more auditable**, not proven more profitable, than inferring a secret AI from a winning wallet. Related-market/negative-risk portfolios are excluded initially because semantic and conversion errors can manufacture apparent arbitrage.

The prospective rules are `quant-prospective-protocol.json`. It was locally written and hashed before a new two-market snapshot. It is not an externally timestamped immutable preregistration. Preserve its exact hash in a durable commit before any future outcomes are evaluated. No realized return or paper P&L was recorded. The first two-market convenience sample cannot support statistical or global market claims.

The future study has not started: freeze 100 markets, use ask-side depth and exact fees, include unavailable/failed books, impose venue minimums, halve displayed depth, stress prices 1% per leg, and check whether a candidate persists at the next snapshot. Success requires thirty complete qualifying observations across three dates plus independent payoff/permission review. Even success would justify further **paper** work, never self-authorize live trades. No scheduling or always-on runtime is claimed.

Initial collection is recorded in `quant-public-snapshot.json`; consult its actual HTTP results and market constraints. A hash of a response establishes byte identity, not authenticity of every economic claim, complete market coverage or an achievable fill. The initial endpoint reachability check made one additional read-only GET before the frozen feasibility snapshot and was not used for return evaluation.

**Completed feasibility result:** all nine snapshot API GETs returned HTTP 200. The two frozen-discovery markets had minimum size **5 per leg**. Both books were collected sequentially, so synchronized execution cannot be inferred.

## One new failed internal test: paper-result inflation

Source: `src/quant-paper-ledger.mjs` on the main SHA above. `markPaperDecision` does not check an exit quote's observation time against `markedAt`. A synthetic hostile repro used an entry price of 1 observed at `2026-10-23T00:00:00Z`, a mark at `00:01:00Z`, and an exit price of 100 marked as observed in 2030. It was accepted as a valid two-record chain with a 99 fractional paper return. **This is a deliberately fabricated fixture exposing a bug, never an economic result.**

The exact red repro is preserved in `quant-paper-ledger-red.mjs`; it is expected to fail its assertion on this main SHA. The parent was notified to delegate a focused repair through Chat on Steroids. Proposed requirements: finite timestamps, each exit quote observed no later than the mark and not before its entry quote, and explicit invalid-capital/cost guards. No source fix was made by this research lane. Check the later implementation receipt before claiming repair.
