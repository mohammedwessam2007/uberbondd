# 05 — Zero-Loss Quality and Crown Succession

## Quality law

~~~
ABSOLUTE_FRONTIER_QUALITY_DELTA = 0
~~~

Operational meaning: UberMind may reduce cost only through mechanisms that do not intentionally reduce required final quality.

Finite tests cannot prove future equivalence forever. Operational zero-loss therefore requires:
- fresh paired non-regression;
- explicit applicability bounds;
- current provenance;
- continuous shadow audits;
- immediate decompilation on drift/regression.

## Moving Crown

There is no permanent favorite model.

~~~
Crown(taskClass, t)
= strongest verified current market intelligence for that class at time t
~~~

## Crown succession

~~~
new model
 -> quarantine
 -> identity/callability/pricing verification
 -> fresh hidden task battery
 -> task-class paired evaluation
 -> adversarial evaluation
 -> incumbent comparison
 -> Crown/Pantheon promotion if evidence warrants
~~~

Cost does not choose the Crown. Quality does.

## Crown succession invalidates assumptions

When the Crown changes:
- important Jev circuits return to shadow sampling;
- compressed architectures are rechecked;
- reasoning-effort policies are rechecked;
- old frontier-equivalent claims become historical until refreshed.

UberMind therefore inherits future frontier improvements.

## Champion-Challenger Market

Per task archetype maintain:
- production champion architecture;
- one or more shadow challengers;
- direct Crown baseline samples;
- current cost and quality evidence.

A challenger may use:
- fewer agents;
- different cheap lineages;
- more Jev decisions;
- different evidence ordering;
- smaller Crown delta;
- same-model Batch/deferred;
- new provider route.

Promotion requires equal-or-better required quality evidence. Lower cost alone is insufficient.

## Clean baseline

In burn-in:
- Crown answer is generated independently;
- cheap workers do not steer the baseline before it exists;
- final synthesis attacks a preserved reference.
