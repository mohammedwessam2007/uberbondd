## Purpose

This file prevents two opposite failures:

1. pretending every useful component is unprecedented;
2. flattening the full UberMind synthesis into "just another router."

The standard is:

- identify public neighbors;
- name what they already contribute;
- import useful donor mechanisms;
- define the remaining synthesis precisely;
- make novelty and superiority empirical questions.

## Strongest research hypothesis

The strongest defensible hypothesis is:

> A moving external intelligence frontier can be exposed as a stable logical service and implemented by a profile-guided, quality-typed cognitive compiler that progressively lowers verified recurring cognition through exact reuse, bounded semantic reflexes and deterministic execution, while residualizing novel semantics upward and recompiling when the frontier or reality changes.

## Required ablation program

1. direct Crown only;
2. ordinary model router;
3. router + adaptive debate;
4. router + Jev;
5. router + exact reuse;
6. V5 without cross-task CSE;
7. V5 without residualization;
8. V5 without Jev tier;

Measure:

- paired quality;
- cost;
- frontier tokens;
- latency;
- founder time;
- semantic page faults;
- deoptimizations;
- routing regret;
- reuse multiplicity;
- compilation survival after Crown succession.

## Publishability gate

The strongest credible result would show:

```
same or better paired required quality
+
lower all-in cost
+
lower frontier-token share over repeated use
+
successful deoptimization on drift
+
successful recompilation after a stronger Crown arrives
+
ablation evidence that the compiler mechanisms caused the savings
```

A one-shot cheap benchmark win is not enough.

UberMind V5 is testing a broader synthesis: frontier intelligence virtualization through quality-typed cognitive compilation, semantic reflexes, residual frontier reasoning, amortization, and continuous recompilation against a moving market baseline.

If the experiments are weak, shrink the claim.

If the experiments are strong, let the receipts carry the adjective.
