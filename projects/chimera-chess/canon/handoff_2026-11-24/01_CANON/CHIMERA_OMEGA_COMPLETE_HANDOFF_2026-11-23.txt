05. BASELINE ARCHITECTURE: WHAT THE ENGINE ACTUALLY WOULD BE
================================================================================

[DESIGN] CHIMERA has one fast shared neural chess representation, conditioned
by compact style/plan states, plus optional bounded numerical chess search.

A. Input:
   - Legal board position including side to move, castling rights, en passant,
     clocks and relevant game history.
   - Persistent strategic objective/plan representation (if any).
   - Rule-of-chess enforcement, no hallucinated illegal moves.
   - Optional authorized opponent distribution model, NOT private profiling.

B. Geometry-aware shared encoder:
   - 64-square piece tokens or an efficient board embedding representation;
     alternative: NNUE-like features for lowest latency. Compare experimentally.
   - Context about king safety, pawn structure, piece relationships, target
     square complexes, material, move history.

C. Output heads from ONE shared pass:
   - Human-inspired candidate move policy / one or several diverse policies.
   - Expected value / WDL probability / score estimate.
   - Confidence and calibration / expected value of more search.
   - Future plan, necessary preconditions, likely opposing countermoves.
   - Historical style dimensions and learned independent 'sixth instinct'.
   - Intermediate-layer candidate rescue (only if proven beneficial).

D. Strategic memory:
   - Small latent vector tracking intent, critical prerequisites and what was
     invalidated by the opponent's last move.
   - Re-evaluate, abandon or transform plans when evidence changes.
   - Avoid independent rapid switching between incompatible personalities.

E. Selective computation:
   - Easy position: one fast neural pass and legal-move checks.
   - High-uncertainty or tactically dangerous position: additional candidates,
     shallow/targeted search, or a bounded Stockfish/Leela critic.
   - Asymmetric compute allocation based on actual value of information,
     not arbitrary human-looking delays.

F. Oracle-Heretic system:
   - Stockfish: strongest currently identified lines, objective tactical
     counterargument, MultiPV candidate comparisons, optional evaluation.
   - Heretic/Artist: original but plausible alternatives.
   - Assassin: most dangerous opposing response, refutation.
   - Human Mirror: estimated human choice diversity, only for evaluation and
     legitimate test play, not detector evasion.
   - Historian: plan consistency across the game.
   - Oracle of Regret: estimated loss in expected results if choosing creative
     alternative instead of objective best.
   - Final selection is not simply the highest Stockfish score; evaluate the
     calibrated strength-creativity Pareto frontier.

G. Output:
   - Legal selected move, optionally an interpretable short plan summary.
   - No statement that a verbalized summary necessarily corresponds exactly
     to neural internal reasoning. Faithfulness requires separate tests.

H. Offline training laboratory:
   - Historical PGNs + legitimate annotation rights + source provenance.
   - Deep tactical teacher/search, original motif counterfactual data.
   - Quality-diversity evolutionary research + self-play + ablation.
   - Expensive world-model planning and diffusion generation if useful.
   - Distill to fast mobile model and verify what is actually retained.

CAUTION: A multi-head model with a Stockfish subprocess is still a chess engine.
It is not a biological mind. Novelty and chess understanding require tests.

06. COMPLETE NUMBERED INVENTION CATALOG: ORIGINAL IDEAS 01-45
================================================================================

All following entries are DESIGN PROPOSALS. The number is a conversation-design
identifier; not a patent, publication, implementation, verified unique invention,
or a mapping to any UberBond idea identifier.

01. FUTURE SCULPTOR
    Envision valuable configurations several moves into the future and plan
    backward toward them. Train with verified reachable target positions;
    do not generate fantasies without legal paths. Runtime: small plan head.

02. PARALLEL IMAGINATION CHAMBER
    Attacking, positional, tactical, experimental and skeptical expert
    perspectives propose and challenge plans. Distill debate into shared
    representation; don't run five full models by default on device.

03. DELAYED REVELATION
    Seek plans composed of unthreatening preparatory moves whose purpose
    emerges later. Verify coordination and necessity, not merely surprise.

04. STRATEGIC METAMORPHOSIS ENGINE
    Learn to transform between eccentric opening, restrictive middlegame,
    tactical attack and technical ending, based on positional evidence.

05. ORACLE OF REGRET
    Estimate opportunity cost of a creative move versus an objectively
    stronger alternative using calibrated WDL, not just centipawns.

06. BEAUTIFUL REFUTATION GENERATOR
    Produce attractive but unsound combinations during offline training,
    discover strongest defenses, and learn exact failure conditions.

07. DISAGREEMENT LABORATORY
    Save positions where intuitive policy, causal planner and Stockfish
    diverge; label causes of disagreement, train on useful exceptions.

08. COUNTERFACTUAL BLACKSMITH
    Modify the *earlier* position that prevented a sacrifice from working;
    learn to create those preconditions through playable moves.

09. TEMPTATION CARTOGRAPHER
    Model likely response distribution of strong human players from lawful
    historical/consented data. Scientifically test practical difficulty;
    avoid personal private profiling or enforcement-evasion objectives.

10. HYDRA ARCHITECT
    Construct positions with multiple independent, mutually reinforcing
    threats or latent plans; prefer redundancy over single fragile attacks.

11. COUNTERINTUITIVE POSITION GENERATOR
    Discover novel yet defensible position families and the route to them.
    Test objectively against strong defense and diverse opponents.

12. FALSE-SAFETY RESEARCHER
    Study positions that look comfortable to humans but demand subtle
    calculation; test assumption using controlled human study.

13. ATTENTION-CONFLICT MODEL
    Find positions requiring attention to unrelated strategic fronts,
    e.g. king danger plus distant passed pawn. Test whether this truly
    raises error rates, rather than assuming complexity equals difficulty.

14. PSYCHOLOGICAL COMPLEXITY ATLAS
    Index position classes by observed human error rates, response entropy,
    unfamiliarity, time and actual chess difficulty; offline asset.

15. STRATEGIC AUTOBIOGRAPHY
    Track compact intent across moves: the chosen square complex, reason
    for pawn pushes, threats, what changed; generated prose is secondary.

16. CREATIVE OBSESSION
    Allow strong learned preference for beautiful plans, sometimes leading
    to naturally imperfect overcommitment, not scripted blunder schedules.

17. METACOGNITIVE GOVERNOR
    Predict confidence, tactical danger, novelty and expected value of
    additional thought; allocate actual compute adaptively.

18. MUSEUM OF GLORIOUS FAILURES
    Catalog characteristic flawed ideas and the misconceptions behind them;
    learn generalizable corrective concepts.

19. SIXTH INSTINCT NURSERY / UNBORN GRANDMASTER
    Train an independent expert on positions where historical styles
    disagree; reward verified strong, strategically distinct third options.

20. STYLE GENOME
    Continuous latent axes for initiative, restraint, structural oddity,
    risk, long-term orientation, defensive imagination and tactical taste.

21. CHESS AESTHETIC CRITIC
    Learn from high-quality human expert judgments of beauty, surprise,
    depth, sacrifice, coordination and quiet-move elegance. Calibrate bias.

22. ORIGINALITY AUDITOR
    Compare proposals against historical corpus and standard engine lines;
    novelty must be paired with soundness and out-of-sample transfer.

23. EVOLUTIONARY TOURNAMENT
    Develop policy descendants with distinct traits; objectively select
    useful behaviors against diverse opponents and distill back into core.

24. DREAM LABORATORY
    Generate unfamiliar strategic puzzles and hypothetical states OFFLINE;
    validate reachability, legality, soundness and novelty before training.

25. SEARCH-TO-INSTINCT DISTILLATION
    Train a neural model to predict conclusions of expensive verified search
    in one/few forward passes; compare distillation gains against search cost.

26. TACTICAL DNA RECOMBINATION
    Recombine causal building blocks of famous motifs (open lines, pinned
    defenders, weak squares, exchange compensation) into new verified motifs.

27. MISSING INGREDIENT DETECTOR
    Compare near-identical positions where an idea works/fails; identify
    necessary board features and possible ways to create them.

28. COMPRESSED IMAGINATION LIBRARY
    Distill millions of verified offline discoveries into neural weights,
    optionally retain a small structure-based cache.

29. STRATEGIC MUTATION OPERATOR
    Mutate plans during training (rook lift -> bishop shift, side switch,
    structure modification); keep only robust variants.

30. UNSEEN MOTIF HUNTER
    Quality-diversity archiving: cluster verified unusual ideas by chess
    mechanism to avoid rediscovering the same novelty repeatedly.

31. THREE-FUTURES INSTINCT
    From one shared encoder predict tactical attack, positional conversion,
    and favorable endgame prospects; avoid three complete networks.

32. QUIET-MOVE GENIUS
    Explicitly learn stunning non-check, non-capture, non-threat positional
    choices; prevent the model equating creativity with sacrifices alone.

33. POSITIONAL PARADOX DETECTOR
    Discover when conventionally unattractive structural traits provide
    important dynamic compensation; require causal explanation/test.

34. BEAUTY-FROM-DEFENSE GENERATOR
    Train on remarkable defensive moves, counter-sacrifices, fortress
    ideas, unexpected resources; preserve artistry under pressure.

35. SINGLE-PASS MULTI-INSTINCT HEADS
    One expensive shared inference, several small experts/heads; selective
    routing of candidate styles instead of running full expert models.

36. INCREMENTAL CHESS PERCEPTION
    Reuse board feature computations between moves where architecture
    permits, inspired by incremental NNUE updates; validate correctness.

37. INSTANT REGRET ESTIMATOR
    Cheap learned approximation of how much objective win probability a
    creative candidate sacrifices, with optional deeper critic triggered.

38. SPARSE IMAGINATION ACTIVATION
    Call enhanced creative mechanisms only on high-value or high-uncertainty
    moves; benchmark gating errors and compute savings.

39. SUPPRESSED GENIUS DETECTOR
    Examine intermediate neural layers for promising alternatives lost
    before final policy; train low-cost probes and check whether extracted
    candidate moves have actual tactical quality. Research-inspired,
    but meaningful intermediate decoding is NOT automatic.

40. CAUSAL CHESS IMAGINATION ENGINE
    Formalize future objective -> prerequisites -> refutations -> actions
    to establish prerequisites. Train using causal contrasts and legal
    reachability, NOT only attractive target-state pictures.

41. STRATEGIC TENSION COMPOSER
    Learn when to accumulate, transform or release positional tension;
    dynamic positional complexity as a controlled resource. Distinguish
    causal advantage from metric artifacts.

42. PERSONALITY CONTINUITY MECHANISM
    Persistent strategic latent state prevents incoherent abrupt switching
    between master-style advisors; update or abandon plan on evidence.

43. EVOLUTIONARY MUSEUM OF UNBORN STRATEGIES
    Offline quality-diversity generation of previously unclassified robust
    strategic concepts; archive, audit originality and distill discoveries.

44. CHESS INTUITION AT THREE TIMESCALES
    Shared encoder + immediate threats, medium-term strategic trajectories,
    and long-term structural/precondition prediction heads.

45. EXPERT'S MISTAKE LABORATORY
    Matched successful/failed strategic plans reveal model misconceptions.
    Target calibrated fallibility rather than random errors.

07. OTHER IMPORTANT UNNUMBERED MECHANISMS AND TERMINOLOGY
================================================================================

THE PARADOX WEAVER:
  Positions that appear absurd or contradictory under standard positional
  heuristics but contain a coherent, verified strategic purpose.

THE INVERSE CHESS ENGINE:
  Instead of only 'position -> candidate move -> future', also use
  'desired future -> constraints -> preparation -> actionable move'.

THE ORACLE-HERETIC ENGINE:
  Stockfish as objective critic and refutation oracle; neural original
  creative proposal remains distinct. Oracle should expose strongest enemy
  replies, not just score the top line. The 'worst move' requested by founder
  is most useful as worst PLAUSIBLE candidate or most dangerous refutation,
  not trivially the literal worst legal move.

COGNITIVE DISAGREEMENT ENGINE:
  Preserve differences between intuition, strategic prediction and search;
  differences are opportunities for learning, not evidence of correctness.

NEURAL-ORACLE INDEPENDENCE TEST:
  Compare PURE NEURAL, NEURAL + ORACLE, DISTILLED NEURAL as three agents;
  examine strength, style coherence, creativity, mistake mechanism and speed.

COGNITIVE TURING / NEURAL AUTHENTICITY LABORATORY:
  Disclosed offline/blinded research in which consenting titled experts
  assess games from humans, conventional engines and CHIMERA. Measure
  judgment accuracy, reasons, strategic coherence, error type, not success
  at evading a platform's confidential enforcement system.

CREATIVE INTELLIGENCE COMPILER:
  Train many offline specialist 'teachers' and distill useful disagreements,
  failures and concepts into a smaller mobile student.

STRATEGIC AUTOBIOGRAPHY + FAITHFUL INTERPRETABILITY:
  Explanations should correspond to genuinely predictive latent plan states;
  generated plausible stories alone are insufficient.

OPPONENT MODELING:
  Model population-level human choice distributions for objective practical
  difficulty research; earlier Swindlefish discussion mentioned possible
  negative empirical utility, so require benefit tests.

PARETO FRONTIER:
  Compare candidate moves in objective expected score, practical difficulty,
  originality, beauty, and plan coherence; do not sacrifice substantial
  winning chances merely for unusual-looking moves.

STRATEGIC SHAPE-SHIFTING:
  Different master-inspired instincts activate in different phases, while
  strategic memory stops random personality switching.

NATURAL FALLIBILITY:
  Tunnel vision, false intuition, overconfidence, calculation blind spots,
  plan stubbornness, and creative overreach as consequences of limited
  modeling, not synthetic conspicuous blunders.

09. TRAINING DESIGN: HOW TO CONSTRUCT THE NEW CHESS MIND
================================================================================

STAGE 0: DATA AND REPRODUCIBILITY
  Verified PGNs from named masters, opponents and matched controls.
  Ensure metadata, legal move parsing, deduplication, FEN, result and time
  controls. Sources and licenses. Hold out years, opponents and structures.
  Never assume earlier 'all games researched' claim means a usable corpus.

STAGE 1: SHARED CHESS INTUITION
  Train legal-move policy, WDL/value, tactical motifs, board relationships,
  king safety, pawn structure, position reconstruction, and uncertainty.
  Compare a simple NNUE-like baseline and board transformer backbone.

STAGE 2: HUMAN STYLE WITHOUT CONFUSING IT WITH STRENGTH
  Player embeddings and conditioning; predict style residuals controlling
  for Elo, era, opening and opponent. Separate style fidelity and objective
  chess quality. Evaluate unseen games from same and new players.

STAGE 3: CAUSAL TACTICS AND PRECONDITIONS
  Generate matched near-identical positions where a plan works/fails;
  use search to label whether specific pieces/squares/refutations matter.
  Learn 'missing ingredient' representation and plan feasibility.

STAGE 4: COUNTERFACTUAL FUTURES AND CREATIVE DIVERSITY
  Search for unfamiliar but robust motif families and plausible actions
  leading there. Strongest-defense checks, original concepts archive,
  quality-diversity and self-play without rewarding bad novelty.

STAGE 5: ADAPTIVE META-REASONING
  Predict failure risk of initial intuition, expected search improvement,
  plan uncertainty, and compute budget. Store plan state. Test if deeper
  thinking actually improves choices at the same total compute cost.

STAGE 6: ORACLE ADVERSARIAL TEACHER
  Strong Stockfish and/or Leela analysis: MultiPV, tactical refutations,
  win-probability regret, critical positions, negative examples. Teacher
  must not dictate every move or style collapses.

STAGE 7: DISTILLATION AND QUANTIZATION
  Distill research ensemble into 50M, 150M, 300M etc. students as feasible;
  retain a separate smaller fast baseline. Quantize. Benchmark on device.

STAGE 8: INDEPENDENT GAMING AND ARTISTIC VALIDATION
  Play disclosed engine tournaments under identical hardware and time
  controls, use expert reviews of originality, and causal tests. Evaluate
  against equal-strength but conventional policies; not only Stockfish.

STAGE 9: FALSIFICATION AND PRUNING
  Remove every new subsystem in turn. If a simpler policy+search matches
  results, delete ornamental complexity. Measure genuine return per watt,
  ms, parameter and GPU training cost.

11. EVALUATION, FALSIFIERS, AND SCIENTIFIC DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

DIMENSION A: REAL CHESS STRENGTH
  Elo determined under a specific reproducible pool, clock, hardware and
  pairing protocol. Use confidence intervals; distinguish engine Elo from
  human FIDE classical and online blitz scales.

DIMENSION B: CREATIVE QUALITY
  Blind expert judgment of coherent plans, surprising but sound moves,
  quiet positional brilliance, defensive creativity, and aesthetic depth.
  Control for equal playing strength and board difficulty.

DIMENSION C: INDEPENDENT ORIGINALITY
  New motif clusters relative to training set, grandmaster examples and
  strong baseline engine lines. Novel doesn't imply useful or sound.

DIMENSION D: CAUSAL STRATEGIC COMPETENCE
  Matched positions where one feature invalidates a plan. Test whether
  CHIMERA changes move/plan in the predicted direction for the correct
  tactical or positional reason. Use intervention and counterfactuals.

DIMENSION E: PERSISTENT PLAN COHERENCE
  Does strategic plan state predict future choices? Does CHIMERA revise
  its plans appropriately when adversary disrupts them?

DIMENSION F: BOUNDED COGNITION AND NATURAL ERROR
  Examine categories of mistakes and neural confidence calibration.
  Randomly inserted weak moves or cosmetic timing do NOT qualify.

DIMENSION G: REAL-TIME PHONE PERFORMANCE
  Cold vs warm initialization; per-move p50/p95/p99 and throughput;
  continuous 1h+ battery/thermal behavior; memory peak; CPU/NPU reliability.

DIMENSION H: OPPORTUNITY COST
  Does each clever subsystem improve strength, originality or execution
  enough to justify extra latency, training cost and complexity?

KEY ABLATIONS:
  A0: Plain strong compact policy + standard search (minimal baseline).
  A1: A0 + master style conditioning only.
  A2: A1 + future/causal planning.
  A3: A2 + strategic persistent memory.
  A4: A3 + suppressed-genius intermediate proposals.
  A5: A4 + separate Oracle/Regret controller.
  A6: Full offline creativity discovery + distilled student.

MEASURE:
  - Elo with uncertainty, not selected highlight games.
  - Strongest-defense evaluation; tactical blunder rate by position class.
  - Blind originality scores with inter-rater reliability.
  - Novel verified motifs per million training positions.
  - Counterfactual-plan success rate on held-out structures.
  - Latency, temperature, and energy per move on OnePlus 15.
  - Styles blending vs coherent novel identity.
  - Training and data costs, and ease of reproduction.

STRONGEST FAIR FALSIFIERS:
  1. Stockfish/LC0 at equal latency wins easily while being equally creative
     by blinded expert ratings: CHIMERA is ornamental complexity.
  2. Master-style mixing reduces each style's distinctiveness and creates
     strategic incoherence: mixture is inferior to one policy.
  3. 'Causal' model uses superficial correlations and cannot generalize
     to mutated unseen board positions.
  4. Historical 'new' idea is a memorized position or widely known motif.
  5. Neurally generated human-style errors become far too frequent to retain
     elite strength; risk/creativity frontier is unfavorable.
  6. Mobile GPU/NPU launch overhead, heat or memory stalls swamp gains from
     large model size or MoE routing.
  7. Stockfish critic dominates all move selections, making CHIMERA a style
     wrapper and refuting its claimed independent creativity.
  8. Rarity and human difficulty are different: unusual position families
     fail to increase human errors or win rates in consented studies.

  Human cognition inspiration and strong performance may conflict. The AI
  remains an engine; human-like behavior must be operationalized.

  Too much teacher imitation produces engine clone; too little tactical
  verification produces strategically unsound art.

C05. WORLD MODEL VS SEARCH.

  Routing between imitations can look random; continuity mechanism needed.

C07. BEAUTY VS OBJECTIVE WINNING CHANCES.
  Human expert artistry may reward objectively dubious sacrifices; control
  baseline strength and external evaluation.

C08. NOVELTY CLAIM NOT YET VALIDATED.
