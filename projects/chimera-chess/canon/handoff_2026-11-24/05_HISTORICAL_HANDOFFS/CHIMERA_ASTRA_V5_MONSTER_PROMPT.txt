6. MATH, PAPER AND SPECIFICATION CLAIMS THAT REQUIRE FALSIFICATION
================================================================================

Your job includes adversarial scientific skepticism, not passive agreement.
The following specific contradictions must remain visible until resolved:

1. A single 16-bit VQ code cannot be assumed to encode every useful plan;
   65,536 tokens are possible but causal meaning is not guaranteed.
2. SAE feature labels and straight-line activation directions do not prove
   manipulable independent chess concepts; causal intervention controls needed.
3. The Chessformer GAB and DPA terms share a softmax and aren't independently
   orthogonal just because they can be written separately.
4. Maia3-79M's published eight-layer architecture does not have layers 8–16;
   exact model-layer counts must be inspected before writing hook code.
5. A model trained with zero dropout cannot become a meaningful eight-sample
   MC-dropout ensemble by simply toggling training mode.
6. Batch=8 is not necessarily batch=1 latency on mobile hardware; measure.
7. Extra recurrent passes aren't free, and KL convergence between policies
   is not evidence of tactical correctness.
8. A candidate six-ply diffusion rollout is not an adversarial proof of
   a plan: opponent defenses branch and may refute it.
9. WDL variance among candidate moves is not a universal chess-complexity
   measure, and output-head gradients are not inherently true Fisher curvature.
10. Hodge harmonic edge flows do not automatically represent prophylaxis;
    topology depends on the chosen complex and graph-cell definitions.
11. Chess does not in general evolve as an energy-conserving symplectic
    Hamiltonian system. Captures, promotions and adversarial decisions break
    the claimed physical analogy.
12. Sheaf compatibility is not a replacement for chess legality or tactical
    verification; the coboundary/cohomology relationship must be stated right.
13. Truncated level-two path signatures do not uniquely identify cognition.
14. Orthogonality to a historical embedding corpus does not prove conceptual
    novelty or objective chess value.
15. A human move-prediction Top-1 percentage is not a chess Elo rating.
