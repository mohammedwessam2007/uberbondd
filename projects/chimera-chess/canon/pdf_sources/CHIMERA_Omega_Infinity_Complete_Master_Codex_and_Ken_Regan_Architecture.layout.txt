DEVELOP PARANOIA

calculates, they immediately discard moves that leave a Queen or Rook unprotected and spend their
clock defending against concrete checks or captures. CHIMERA Ω∞ systematically exploits four
cognitive vulnerabilities:
 1. Working-Memory Overload ( #184 , #188 , #217 ): By leaving up to three pieces
    simultaneously capturable while every capture is refuted by a distinct 4-to-6 ply mating net,
    CHIMERA forces the human brain to calculate multiple sacrificial trees in parallel, draining 40–
    60 seconds on a single turn.
 2. Loss-Aversion & Confirmation-Bias Hallucination ( #216 ): Using Maia-3 , CHIMERA
    identifies completely aimless quiet moves ( Ra2 , Nh1 , Kf1 ) that provoke a human opponent into
    hallucinating a non-existent attack and making a weakening pawn push ( h6 or g6 ) that loses on
    the spot.
 3. Agency Inversion & Self-Entombment ( #215 ): Instead of capturing the opponent's
    defenders, CHIMERA lures the opponent's own pawns and minor pieces onto all eight squares
    surrounding their King, inducing lasting trauma when they realize they built their own coffin.
 4. Sudden-Death Zero-Warning Execution ( #220 ): By suppressing all non-mating checks for
    25–35 moves, CHIMERA deprives the opponent of the psychological warning signals humans rely
    on to sense danger—until the very first check of the entire game is checkmate ( # ).
