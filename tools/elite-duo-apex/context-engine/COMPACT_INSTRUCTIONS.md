# Compact instructions

Preserve exactly:

1. mission ID, intent, goal, authority hierarchy, constraints, approvals and stop condition;
2. accepted architecture, invariants, state and transaction boundaries;
3. changed files and exact identifiers;
4. completed and failed tests with commands and evidence paths;
5. unresolved P0/P1 findings and blockers;
6. rejected paths that must not be reopened without new evidence;
7. external actions, including confirmed, failed, pending and unknown outcomes;
8. current phase and next finite action;
9. rollback and protected paths;
10. artifact paths and hashes needed for rehydration.

Do not preserve routine narration, obsolete logs, repeated explanations, or abandoned implementation details.
