# Failure modes

1. Optimizing a local international expansion prep metric while breaking the end-to-end outcome.
2. Duplicating authority or state inside the international expansion prep workflow.
3. Testing an abstraction without proving operational reachability.
4. Using stale evidence or configuration at the final action boundary.
5. Declaring readiness without rollback, monitoring, or residual-risk disclosure.
6. Allowing untrusted content to redefine mission authority.
