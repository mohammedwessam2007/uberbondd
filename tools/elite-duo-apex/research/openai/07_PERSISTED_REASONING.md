# Persisted reasoning pattern

**Source basis:** `OAI-56-GUIDE`  
**Status:** source-derived public guidance, verified 2026-08-01

## Findings

- Reasoning can persist across turns when goals and assumptions remain stable.
- Current-turn context is better when prior reasoning is obsolete.
- Multi-turn continuity must preserve relevant response items and IDs.

## ELITE DUO implementation

V2 persists explicit decision artifacts and starts fresh when assumptions change.
