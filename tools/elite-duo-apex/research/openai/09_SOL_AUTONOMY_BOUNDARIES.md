# Autonomy boundaries

**Source basis:** `OAI-56-GUIDE`  
**Status:** source-derived public guidance, verified 2026-08-01

## Findings

- Reading, diagnosing, planning, building, and fixing authorize different actions.
- Safe local work should not require repeated approval.
- External, destructive, costly, or scope-expanding actions require confirmation.
- State the policy once.

The mission compiler classifies intent and the hooks enforce the boundary.
